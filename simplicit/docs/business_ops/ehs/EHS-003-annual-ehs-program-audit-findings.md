## 3. 2025 Audit Findings — Detail and 2026 Status

### HIGH PRIORITY FINDINGS (2025)

**Finding H-01: LOTO Program — Inadequate Equipment-Specific Procedures**  
- **Clause:** OSHA 1910.147(c)(4)(i)  
- **Observation:** 14 of 38 machines requiring LOTO procedures lacked equipment-specific written procedures. Operators were using a generic LOTO procedure instead.  
- **2025 Corrective Action:** Engineering to develop equipment-specific LOTO procedures for all 38 machines.  
- **2026 Status:** ✅ COMPLETE — All 38 machine-specific LOTO procedures developed, reviewed, and posted. Re-training of 142 affected employees completed March 2026.

**Finding H-02: Machine Guarding — Missing Guards on 6 Machines**  
- **Clause:** OSHA 1910.212(a)(1)  
- **Observation:** Point-of-operation guards missing or removed on 6 machines across Plant 1 (2) and Plant 2 (4). Three machines tagged as "guard removed for maintenance — not returned."  
- **2025 Corrective Action:** Immediate LOTO of affected machines; guards replaced or fabricated.  
- **2026 Status:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY CLOSED — 4 of 6 machines closed in 2025. Two Plant 2 machines (including Deburring Stations 4 and 6) had guards go missing again in early 2026, contributing to INC-2026-007. Both re-guarded May 23, 2026. Machine guarding inspection added to monthly safety walk checklist.

**Finding H-03: Incident Investigation — Root Cause Not Documented for 3 of 5 Incidents**  
- **Clause:** Internal EHS program requirement; industry best practice  
- **Observation:** 2024 first aid and near-miss investigations lacked documented root cause analysis. Investigations were limited to description of event and immediate corrective action.  
- **2025 Corrective Action:** EHS training for supervisors on 5-Why root cause methodology; new incident investigation form deployed.  
- **2026 Status:** ✅ COMPLETE — All 2025 and 2026 investigations include documented root cause. INC-2026-007 investigation (EHS-002) demonstrates robust 5-Why analysis.

---

### MEDIUM PRIORITY FINDINGS (2025)

**Finding M-01: PPE — No Documented PPE Hazard Assessment**  
- **Clause:** OSHA 1910.132(d)  
- **Observation:** Simplicit had no written, certified PPE hazard assessment for any of its work areas. PPE requirements were communicated informally.  
- **2026 Status:** ✅ COMPLETE — Written PPE hazard assessment completed for all 18 work areas (January 2026). Certified by EHS Manager. Posted in each area.

**Finding M-02: HazCom — 12 Chemicals Without Current SDS**  
- **Clause:** OSHA 1910.1200(g)  
- **Observation:** Safety Data Sheets for 12 chemical products were either missing or older than the current manufacturer version.  
- **2026 Status:** ✅ COMPLETE — All SDS updated; transition to electronic SDS management system (Cority) completed February 2026.

**Finding M-03: Forklift — 3 Operators Not Current on Annual Evaluation**  
- **Clause:** OSHA 1910.178(l)(4)(ii)  
- **2026 Status:** ✅ COMPLETE — All 28 operators current as of Q1 2026 re-certification.

**Finding M-04: Emergency Action Plan — Employee Notification System Untested**  
- **Observation:** Emergency notification system (PA/alarm) had not been tested for 18 months.  
- **2026 Status:** ✅ COMPLETE — Full evacuation drill conducted February 2026 (Plant 1) and March 2026 (Plant 2). PA system tested and functional.

**Finding M-05: Walking/Working Surfaces — Aisle Markings Faded in 4 Areas**  
- **2026 Status:** ✅ COMPLETE — Aisle re-marking project completed April 2026.
