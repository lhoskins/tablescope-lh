### EPRJ-004 — Defense Connector Qualification

**Lead Engineer:** Chen Wei
**Customer:** Cascade Defense Systems (CUS003)
**Budget:** $310K | **Spent:** $185.8K | **% Consumed:** 59.9% | **Status: Green**

**Budget tracking:** 59.9% consumed at 55% of project duration. Slight positive trend; project is expected to complete within budget.

### EPRJ-005 — Medical Implant Prototype

**Lead Engineer:** Dana Kovacs
**Budget:** $450K | **Spent:** $336.4K | **% Consumed:** 74.8% | **Status: Yellow**

**Q2 Progress:**
- Prototype machining of 6 implant geometries completed in April using Harbor Steel specialty alloy (ASTM F316L stainless).
- Dimensional inspection of all 6 prototypes: 4 passed; 2 failed radii tolerances on profile. Kovacs is leading redesign iteration on the 2 failures.
- Biocompatibility testing submitted to SGS Health Science (external lab); results expected August 2026.
- ISO 10993 documentation in preparation; target completion October 2026.

**Cost Status (Yellow):** At 74.8% budget consumed with approximately 35–40% of project duration remaining. Kovacs' estimate-at-completion (EAC) projects $462K total spend — a $12K overrun on $450K budget (2.7%). New Engineering governance (ENG-001 Section 6) requires formal scope change request if spend is projected >5% over; currently below that threshold but being monitored.

**Risks:**
- Customer (Harbor Medical Devices, CUS008) has indicated their FDA submission timeline may slip 2 months. If so, project completion target of December 2026 may slip to February 2027, with corresponding holdover costs.
- The design iteration on the 2 failed prototypes adds an estimated $18K in machining and inspection cost not originally budgeted. This will push the project to a formal overrun request if confirmed.
