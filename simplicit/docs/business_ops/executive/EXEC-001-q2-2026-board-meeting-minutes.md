## 3. CEO Report — Strategic Update (Daniel Okafor)

Daniel Okafor presented a summary of Q2 2026 performance and strategic context.

**Highlights:**
- Q2 revenue of $21.8M came in approximately 8% below the full-year quarterly run-rate target of $23.7M, driven by a combination of booking slippage in April–May and the ongoing Line 2 scrap quality issue that affected throughput in June.
- The five strategic initiatives remain active; two are on track, two are experiencing execution risk, and one (ERP upgrade) is progressing on revised scope.
- The CEO acknowledged that Q1 attrition in Manufacturing (13 separations in a 160-person department over the quarter) has introduced capacity risk heading into H2.

**Key Message from CEO:** "We have a solid backlog and strong customer relationships. The near-term challenge is operational execution — specifically Line 2 scrap, material cost inflation, and stabilizing our manufacturing workforce. The leadership team has developed mitigation plans for each, and I will walk the board through those plans today."

**Board Discussion:** Director Nair asked whether Line 2 scrap had been isolated to a specific supplier lot. Okafor confirmed the root cause investigation points to incoming material specification variance from Cascade Alloys (SUP003), and that a formal 8D corrective action has been issued. Quality Director Robinson provided supplemental detail during the Q&A segment.

## 4. Financial Performance — Q2 2026 (Richard Lewis, CFO)

### 4a. Q2 Income Statement Summary

| Metric | Q2 2026 Budget | Q2 2026 Actual | Variance | % Var |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $23.7M | $21.8M | $(1.9M) | (8.0)% |
| Gross Margin | 38.0% | 36.4% | (1.6 pp) | — |
| EBITDA | 14.0% | 12.1% | (1.9 pp) | — |
| Direct Material Cost | $8.4M | $9.3M | $(0.9M) | (10.3)% |
| Engineering Project Spend | $1.2M | $1.5M | $(0.3M) | (25.0)% |
| SG&A | $3.1M | $3.0M | $0.1M | 3.2% |

**CFO Commentary:** The two primary negative drivers in Q2 are (1) direct material cost inflation, primarily titanium alloys and specialty steel, running 10.3% over budget, and (2) engineering project overruns on EPRJ-003 (Line 2 Process Optimization, 103.8% of budget consumed) and EPRJ-007 (Titanium Alloy Study, 117.6% of budget consumed). Revenue shortfall is secondary but masks an underlying booking problem in April–May that the VP Sales will address.

### 4b. Cash Position and Working Capital

- Operating cash: $4.2M (vs. $5.1M target)
- AR Days Outstanding: 48 days (vs. 42-day target); two Platinum customers on extended terms
- Inventory turns: 5.8x (vs. 6.5x target); elevated WIP tied to Line 2 rework

**Audit Committee Chair Kuchta** asked whether the AR aging had any concentration risk. CFO Lewis confirmed that Bolt Dynamics Inc (CUS002, $2.3M YTD) has a 60-day payment history and no overdue balance; the AR aging issue relates to timing of invoicing, not collectibility.

### 4c. Full-Year Forecast Revision

CFO Lewis tabled a memo (FIN-003) revising the FY2026 full-year EBITDA forecast from 14.0% to 12.8%, a reduction of 120 basis points, citing material costs and sales slippage. Mitigation actions include a supplier repricing negotiation with top 5 vendors and accelerated H2 revenue recovery plan.

**Motion (Audit Committee):** Kuchta moved to accept the revised FY2026 forecast as a planning basis, with the understanding that management will report monthly on material cost trajectory.
**Second:** Ferreira
**Vote:** 6-0, approved.
