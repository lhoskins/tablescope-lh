## Initiative Detail

### STR-001 — Digital Transformation / Manufacturing Execution System (MES)

**Owner:** Susan Moore (COO) / Ashley Rodriguez (IT Director)
**Status:** Yellow
**Phase:** Vendor Selection / RFP Evaluation

**Summary:**
The MES initiative aims to replace the current paper-based shop-floor tracking system with a real-time digital production management platform integrated with the upgraded ERP. The initiative was approved in October 2025 with a target go-live of Q1 2027.

**Q2 Progress:**
- RFP issued to four vendors in March 2026; vendor demonstrations completed in April–May.
- Evaluation scoring completed June 10. Top two finalists: Plex Systems and Epicor MES.
- Final vendor selection delayed 6 weeks due to resource conflict — IT team allocated to ERP Phase 2 scope expansion. New target decision date: August 1, 2026.
- Business process workshops completed for production scheduling and quality inspection workflows.

**Key Risks:**
- Delay in vendor selection compresses implementation timeline and increases risk of missing Q1 2027 go-live.
- Line 2 scrap investigation has surfaced additional data capture requirements (real-time SPC for incoming materials) that may expand MES scope.

**Mitigation:** COO Moore has temporarily backstopped IT resources by deferring two lower-priority IT projects (network refresh, secondary data center upgrade). Vendor decision to proceed no later than August 15.

### STR-002 — New Product Line: Next-Generation Aerospace Bracket (EPRJ-001)

**Owner:** Kevin Davis (Engineering Director) / Darnell Washington (Lead Engineer)
**Status:** Green
**Phase:** Design Verification / NPI Gate 3

**Summary:**
Development of a high-precision titanium aerospace bracket for Platinum-tier customer Northstar Aerospace (CUS014) and prospective customer Apex Aerospace LLC (CUS001). The program targets Q4 2026 production readiness with a potential revenue opportunity of $2.5M–$3.8M annually at full ramp.

**Q2 Progress:**
- Design verification testing (DVT) completed for primary load configurations in May 2026. Results within specification.
- Tooling orders placed with Keystone Tooling (SUP011) and Eagle Plastics (SUP005); lead times 32–53 days.
- First article inspection (FAI) planned for August 2026.
- EPRJ-001 budget: $380K total, $222K spent (58.5%). On track.
- NPI Gate 3 readiness review scheduled: September 5, 2026.

**Key Risks:**
- Titanium material costs have increased 12% since project budget was set (January 2025). Cost-of-goods modeling is being revised.
- Apex Aerospace contract renewal (RSK-008) in August 2026 is the primary commercial trigger for this program. A delay or loss of the renewal would reduce near-term revenue justification.

**Mitigation:** Darnell Washington is collaborating with Procurement on alternative alloy specifications that maintain structural performance at lower material cost. Results expected by August 2026.

### STR-003 — ERP Modernization (Phase 2 of 4)

**Owner:** Ashley Rodriguez (IT Director)
**Status:** Yellow
**Phase:** Phase 2 — Finance and Procurement Modules

**Summary:**
The ERP modernization program replaces the legacy Infor VISUAL system (end-of-support Q2 2028) with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations. Phase 1 (infrastructure and core data model) was completed October 2025. Phase 2 covers Finance, AP/AR, and Procurement modules.

**Q2 Progress:**
- Phase 2 data migration discovered 14,700 open legacy purchase order records with inconsistent supplier coding. Remediation required a 6-week data cleansing sprint by IT (Kenneth Williams, Barbara Young) and Finance (Sandra Rodriguez, Karen Hernandez).
- Configuration of Finance module: 78% complete as of June 30.
- Procurement module: 45% complete; blocked by Cascade Alloys and Harbor Steel supplier data reconciliation.
- User acceptance testing (UAT) deferred from July to September 2026.
- Budget: $1.45M total program; $780K spent through Phase 2, tracking within revised scope.

**Key Risks:**
- Data quality remediation extended scope by ~$95K (board-approved June 25).
- Change management readiness: Finance team survey (June 12) shows 58% of users feel "adequately prepared" for new system, below the 75% target. Gap is concentrated in AP processing workflows.
- ERP delay creates downstream risk for MES integration (STR-001), which requires ERP master data to be stable before MES configuration can begin.

**Mitigation:** Ashley Rodriguez has contracted 2 additional implementation consultants from Microsoft SI partner (Avanade) through Q3 2026. Change management workshop series for Finance team begins July 14.

**Phase:** Defense Connector Qualification — Testing

Qualification of a new precision defense connector product for Department of Defense supply chain programs. Target customer: Cascade Defense Systems (CUS003, $2.48M YTD, Platinum). The program builds on the existing defense relationship and targets ITAR-controlled product line expansion.

**Q2 Progress:**

### STR-005 — Workforce Stabilization

**Owner:** Thomas Gonzalez (VP Human Resources)
**Status:** Red
**Phase:** Root Cause / Intervention Design

**Summary:**
In response to an attrition spike in Q1 2026 — particularly in Manufacturing (13 separations January–March) — the Workforce Stabilization initiative was formally activated in April 2026. The initiative targets voluntary turnover reduction from the current annualized rate of approximately 22% in Manufacturing to below 12% by year-end.

**Q2 Progress:**
- Exit interview analysis completed (HR-002). Primary themes: compensation below market (cited by 64% of exiting Manufacturing employees), lack of career advancement clarity (52%), and working conditions on Line 2 and Line 3 during the scrap remediation period (38%).
- Compensation benchmarking study commissioned with Radford Associates; preliminary results indicate Manufacturing base wages are 7–11% below Columbus metro median for comparable roles.
- Retention bonus program proposal prepared for board approval.
- Open requisitions as of June 30: 17 in Manufacturing (up from 8 at start of year).

**Key Risks:**
- Continued attrition in Manufacturing creates a compounding risk: departing experienced operators reduce throughput and raise scrap rates, which further degrades working conditions, accelerating further attrition.
- 4 additional Manufacturing employees flagged as high flight risks in June mid-year calibration (HR-003).

**Mitigation:** Gonzalez has proposed an interim shift differential increase (approved by COO Moore, June 20), effective July 1. Full wage adjustment program pending board approval. Recruiting pipeline is being expanded via agency partnerships (Express Employment, ProSource).
