## Executive Summary

Q2 2026 results reflect meaningful divergence from plan on both revenue and cost dimensions. Total operating expense ran $1.2M over budget, driven by two primary factors: (1) direct material cost inflation of 10.3% above plan, and (2) engineering project overruns on EPRJ-003 and EPRJ-007. Revenue came in $1.9M below the $23.7M Q2 target. EBITDA of 12.1% is 190 basis points below plan and represents the third consecutive quarter of margin compression.

## Section 3: Direct Material Cost Overrun (+10.3%)

**Overrun Amount: $867K vs. plan**

This is the most significant single cost variance in Q2. The overrun is driven by:

| Material Category | Plan ($K) | Actual ($K) | Variance ($K) | % Over |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Titanium Alloys (Cascade Alloys SUP003) | 2,140 | 2,580 | (440) | (20.6)% |
| Specialty Steel (Harbor Steel SUP008, Acme Metal SUP001) | 3,610 | 3,890 | (280) | (7.8)% |
| Fasteners and Brackets | 1,620 | 1,710 | (90) | (5.6)% |
| Consumables / Other | 1,036 | 1,093 | (57) | (5.5)% |
| **Total** | **8,406** | **9,273** | **(867)** | **(10.3)%** |

**Analysis:**
Titanium alloy pricing surged in Q1 2026 following supply disruptions in Australian mining operations. Cascade Alloys (SUP003) invoked a force majeure price adjustment clause in March 2026, adding approximately 18% to per-pound costs. Simplicit's contracts with Cascade Alloys contain quarterly repricing provisions; the FY2025 contract rate was not extended.

Additionally, the Line 2 scrap investigation revealed that incoming material lots from Cascade Alloys in March–April 2026 had specification variance (hardness out of tolerance), resulting in higher-than-expected scrap and rework costs. The combined effect of price inflation and scrap/rework premium on titanium accounts for $440K of the $867K total materials overrun.

**Mitigation Actions:**
1. Procurement has initiated repricing negotiations with Cascade Alloys; targeting a 6-month price protection agreement.
2. Secondary supplier qualification (Ironside Materials, SUP009) underway; expected Q4 2026.
3. Engineering is evaluating alternative alloy specifications on applicable products (EPRJ-007 scope).

## Section 4: Engineering Project Overruns

**Total Engineering Project Spend Overrun: $(302K), or 25.2% above plan**

### EPRJ-003 — Line 2 Process Optimization

| | Budget | Spent (Q2 cumulative) | % |
|---|---|---|---|
| EPRJ-003 | $220,000 | $228,400 | **103.8%** |

**Overrun Detail:** EPRJ-003 was initiated June 2025 to optimize Line 2 throughput and reduce scrap. The project encountered scope expansion when the Line 2 scrap investigation (initiated Q1 2026) revealed that the root cause was partially attributable to incoming material specification variance rather than line process issues alone. This required additional engineering analysis, equipment sensor calibration, and collaboration with the supplier corrective action process. Design iterations on the closed-loop SPC monitoring system added approximately $18K in engineering labor.

### EPRJ-007 — Titanium Alloy Study

| | Budget | Spent (Q2 cumulative) | % |
|---|---|---|---|
| EPRJ-007 | $180,000 | $211,600 | **117.6%** |

**Overrun Detail:** The Titanium Alloy Study was approved in February 2026 to evaluate alternative alloy compositions for cost reduction. The study scope expanded significantly when the Cascade Alloys price spike in Q1 2026 added urgency and broadened the evaluation criteria to include supply-chain-qualified alternative sources. Three additional alloy variants were added to the test matrix. Lab testing costs at OSU Materials Science exceeded original estimates by $31K. The project is now expected to complete by August 31, 2026 (was June 30), with total projected spend of $225K vs. original $180K budget.

**Engineering Overrun Recovery Plan:** Engineering Director Kevin Davis has committed to a revised project governance protocol (monthly written status with Finance co-signature) effective Q3 2026. All projects with spend exceeding 90% of budget will require CFO review before additional expenditure.

## Section 7: Q3 Outlook

Given Q2 results, Finance is revising Q3 internal planning assumptions:

- Revenue target maintained at $23.7M but is dependent on: (a) three slipped Q2 deals closing in July, (b) Apex Aerospace contract renewal confirming, and (c) Line 2 returning to standard throughput by August.
- Material cost inflation expected to moderate to +6% over plan in Q3 if Cascade Alloys repricing negotiation succeeds.
- Engineering project spend expected to normalize in Q3 as EPRJ-003 and EPRJ-007 approach completion.

**Q3 EBITDA scenario:**
- Base: 13.2% (if revenue recovers and materials moderate)
- Downside: 11.5% (if revenue misses and materials remain elevated)

Full-year forecast revision is addressed in FIN-003.
