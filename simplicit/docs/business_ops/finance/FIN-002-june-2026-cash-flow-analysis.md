## 2. Cash Flow Narrative

### Operating Cash Flow — $378K (vs. $618K in May, $782K in June 2025)

Operating cash generation declined 38.8% from May and is $404K below the same month last year. The primary drivers:

**Net Income Decline:** Net income of $385K in June reflects Revenue of $3.67M (below the $3.8M monthly target) at an EBITDA margin of 13.15%, compressed by ongoing direct material cost inflation. The June margin improvement from Q2 lows reflects partial pass-through of material costs to two customers under quarterly index clauses.

**Accounts Receivable Increase (Use of Cash: $287K):**
AR balance grew from $5.19M to $5.48M during June. Key drivers:
- Bolt Dynamics Inc (CUS002): $485K of June invoices billed in the final week; batch processing delay means these will appear in July cash receipts.
- Delta Precision Works (CUS004): $190K overdue balance on invoices 45–60 days old. Finance Manager Rodriguez has initiated a collection call schedule. No impairment risk assessed; Delta has a 6-year payment history.
- AR Days Outstanding (DSO): 48.2 days as of June 30, above the 42-day target and above June 2025's 43.1 days.

**Inventory Increase (Use of Cash: $158K):**
Raw material inventory increased due to: (a) accelerated purchase of specialty steel ahead of anticipated price increases (Acme Metal Supply, PO #2026-0814), and (b) elevated WIP tied to Line 2 quality holds. The Line 2 rework queue held approximately $340K in WIP at month-end, of which $180K was processed and shipped in early July. Finance expects a partial inventory release in July.

**Accounts Payable Increase (Source of Cash: $96K):**
AP balance increased modestly, reflecting the timing of a Cascade Alloys invoice ($380K, due July 15) and normal accrual cycling. Simplicit is current on all supplier payment obligations. Average AP days: 34.

**Commentary:** The current ratio (2.31) remains above covenant minimum (2.10, per bank facility agreement). The quick ratio (1.42) is tightly above the target threshold of 1.40 — Finance is monitoring this metric closely given the elevated inventory levels.

Inventory turns have declined from 6.8x (June 2025) to 5.8x, primarily reflecting the WIP hold on Line 2. If Line 2 scrap is resolved and WIP clears as planned, inventory turns should recover toward 6.3–6.5x by September.

The cash conversion cycle of 54.9 days is approximately 5 days above target, driven primarily by DSO expansion. Finance is working with Sales to enforce invoice delivery within 2 business days of shipment, which is expected to recover 2–3 days of DSO.

## 4. Revolving Credit Facility Status

In June, Simplicit drew $250K on its $8M revolving credit facility (KeyBank, renewed January 2026) to maintain minimum operating cash. This is the first draw on the revolver since Q4 2024.

| Facility Limit | Current Balance | Available | Covenant Headroom |
|---|---|---|---|
| $8,000K | $250K | $7,750K | Current ratio 2.31 vs. 2.10 minimum |

The revolver draw is precautionary. With the Bolt Dynamics AR batch ($485K) expected in the first week of July, Finance anticipates repayment of the revolver draw by July 15, 2026.

**CFO Note:** The revolver draw is not a distress signal; it reflects temporary cash timing. However, two consecutive months of declining operating cash flow (May–June) warrant close monitoring. If Q3 revenue recovery underperforms, a more material revolver draw may be required, which would reduce covenant headroom.
