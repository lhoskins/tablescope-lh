## Purpose

Mid-year calibration sessions ensure consistent application of performance ratings across departments, identify high-potential and flight-risk employees, and align on differentiated actions (retention, development, performance improvement) ahead of H2. This document captures session outcomes and confidential observations.

**Rating Scale Reference:**
- **5 — Exceptional:** Significantly exceeds all expectations; top 5–8% of population
- **4 — Strong:** Consistently exceeds key expectations; targeted for development/promotion
- **3 — Meets Expectations:** Solid contributor; performing at level
- **2 — Development Needed:** Partially meeting expectations; requires active coaching
- **1 — Does Not Meet:** Not meeting expectations; PIP territory

**Target Distribution (Simplicit guideline):**
- 5: ≤8% | 4: 20–25% | 3: 55–60% | 2: 10–15% | 1: ≤3%

## Section 2: Department-Level Calibration Summary

### Engineering

**Participants:** Kevin Davis (Director), George Wright (Manager), Robert Baker (Manager)

**Discussion:** Engineering submitted an initial distribution that was top-heavy — 35% rated 4 or 5. After calibration discussion, Engineering managers revised downward: final distribution is 28% at 4+, more consistent with company norms.

Davis noted that the calibration is challenging because the two most significant engineering projects in H1 (EPRJ-003 and EPRJ-007) are both over budget, making it difficult to give Strong ratings to individuals who worked on those projects without the calibration appearing to reward underperformance. After discussion, the group agreed that individual performance ratings should reflect individual contribution quality and effort, not project outcome — but that rating 4 or 5 requires evidence of specific impact beyond workload completion.

**Flight Risk Flags (Engineering):**
- Employee E-Alpha (Sr Engineering Associate, 4 years tenure): Rated 4. Currently receiving competitive offers; managing project work on EPRJ-007. Davis flagged as highest flight risk. Retention action recommended.
- Employee E-Beta (Engineering Specialist, 5 years tenure): Rated 4. Has expressed interest in broader leadership role; no clear path identified. Moderate flight risk.

**Development Concern (Engineering):**
- Employee E-Gamma (Engineering Analyst, 1.5 years tenure): Rated 2. Struggling with drawing management process and ECR documentation quality. 60-day coaching plan initiated by manager Robert Baker.

### Manufacturing

**Participants:** Kimberly Gonzalez (Director), George Walker (Manager), Christopher Anderson (Manager), Kimberly Thompson (Manager)

**Discussion:** Manufacturing calibration was the most complex session. High attrition in H1 (23 voluntary departures) means that approximately 30% of the current workforce has less than 12 months of tenure, making meaningful performance ratings difficult. The department has a high proportion of 3 ratings by default for newer employees, which is appropriate.

**Calibration Adjustment:** Three supervisors initially rated 4 employees at 5. After discussion, two of those were revised to 4, consistent with the strong-but-not-exceptional distinction.

**Flight Risk Flags (Manufacturing):**
- Employee M-Alpha (Sr Manufacturing Associate, 7 years tenure, Line 2): Rated 4. Multiple recruiters approaching per LinkedIn activity. Critical knowledge holder for Line 2 setup procedures. **Immediate retention action required.** Kimberly Gonzalez to have 1:1 conversation by June 30.
- Employee M-Beta (Manufacturing Coordinator, 5 years tenure): Rated 4. Accepted a counter-offer from a competitor in April and chose to stay; may still be at risk. Monitor closely.
- Employee M-Gamma (Manufacturing Analyst, Plant B): Rated 4. Has asked twice about remote/hybrid flexibility, which is not available for plant roles. Moderate flight risk.
- Employee M-Delta (Manufacturing Specialist, 3 years tenure, Line 3): Rated 3. Exit interview data from departed peers is influencing this employee; conversation about growth and compensation warranted.

**Performance Improvement:**
- Employee M-Epsilon (Manufacturing Coordinator, <1 year tenure): Rated 1. Attendance issues and failure to meet basic production documentation standards. PIP initiated; 30-day review.

### Sales

**Participants:** Kimberly Martinez (Director), Karen Sanchez (Manager), Nancy Baker (Manager), Anthony Harris (Manager)

**Discussion:** Q2 sales performance (15% below plan) complicated the calibration conversation. Martinez argued that some missed quota in Q2 was attributable to market factors (deal slippage, competitive loss) rather than individual rep performance. Gonzalez (HR) facilitated a distinction between "outcome" and "behavior" ratings.

**Calibration Adjustment:** One rep initially rated 5 despite being 22% below year-to-date quota; revised to 4 after discussion, reflecting strong activity metrics but below-expectation outcomes.

**Flight Risk Flags (Sales):**
- Employee S-Alpha (Sales Manager, 4 years tenure): Rated 4. Has expressed frustration with recent competitive losses and questioned company's pricing strategy. Moderate flight risk; important to retain given territory relationships with CUS002 (Bolt Dynamics) and CUS007 (Greenfield Industries).
- Employee S-Beta (Sr Sales Associate, 2 years tenure): Rated 3. Pipeline activity strong but no closed deals YTD; needs coaching on close execution.
