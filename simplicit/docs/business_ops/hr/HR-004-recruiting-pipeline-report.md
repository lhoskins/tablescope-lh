## 3. Direct vs. Agency Channel Analysis

| Channel | Hires YTD | % of Hires | Avg TTF | Agency Fee / Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direct (Internal sourcing, LinkedIn, employee referral) | 9 | 52.9% | 61 days | $0 |
| Agency (ProSource, Express Employment, Robert Half) | 8 | 47.1% | 49 days | $198K (avg $24.8K/hire) |
| Total | 17 | 100% | 56 days | |

**Observations:**
- Agency hires are marginally faster (49 days vs. 61 days) but cost $24.8K per hire on average.
- Employee referrals produce the highest-quality hires (4 of 9 direct hires; 3 are rated 3+ at mid-year); referral bonus ($500 per hired referral) should be increased to incentivize more referrals. Proposal: increase to $1,000 for Manufacturing and Engineering roles.
- Robert Half is being used for Finance and IT roles only; results have been satisfactory.
