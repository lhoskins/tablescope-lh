## 4. Top Recurring Issues

1. **Outlook autodiscover failures on remote VPN** — 34 tickets in Q2. Root cause: DNS configuration conflict introduced in March firewall update. Fix deployed June 18; no recurrence since.
2. **Line 2 shop floor terminals crashing** — 18 tickets in May–June. Related to Line 2 scrap investigation activity (increased data logging frequency overloading legacy terminals). Replacement of 4 terminals approved and scheduled for July.
3. **ERP sandbox access provisioning delays** — 15 tickets. ERP project team access requests being manually provisioned; automation planned for Phase 2 completion.
4. **Laptop battery failures** — 12 tickets across Sales and Engineering. Fleet aging analysis shows 47 laptops exceed 4-year lifecycle; replacement budget request submitted for Q3.
