## 1. Overview

This report summarizes Simplicit Demo Company's semi-annual review of compliance with U.S. export control regulations, including the Export Administration Regulations (EAR, 15 CFR Parts 730–774) administered by the Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) and the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR, 22 CFR Parts 120–130) administered by the Directorate of Defense Trade Controls (DDTC).

---

## 2. Product and Technology Classification

### ITAR-Controlled Products
Simplicit currently has components supplied to one defense customer (Stonebridge Defense Partners) that are classified as defense articles under USML Category XIX (Gas Turbine Engine components — sub-tier supplier role). These parts require:
- ITAR registration (current — Reg. No. M-28471, renewed March 2026, valid through March 2028)
- Technical data controls on associated drawings and specifications
- Export license or applicable exemption for any foreign transfer

**No foreign exports of ITAR-controlled hardware or technical data occurred in H1 2026.**

### EAR-Controlled Products
The majority of Simplicit's products are commercial/industrial precision machined components subject to EAR. Most products are classified as EAR99 (no Export Control Classification Number required). Two product families have been self-classified as ECCN 2B001.b (machine tool components with dimensional accuracy exceeding 6 micron tolerance capability).

| Product Family | ECCN | License Requirement to Most Countries |
|---|---|---|
| General commercial machined parts | EAR99 | No license required |
| High-precision spindle assemblies (tolerance ≤6μm) | 2B001.b | License required for restricted destinations |
| Defense sub-components | ITAR USML XIX | ITAR authorization required |

**No exports of ECCN 2B001.b items to license-required destinations occurred in H1 2026.**

## 6. Audit Findings and Corrective Actions

### Finding 1 — ITAR Clause Missing from Two Customer Agreements
During contract review (see LEG-001), two customer MSAs were found to lack the standard ITAR flow-down clause required when defense articles or technical data may be involved. Both customers are non-defense (commercial only) but the clause is required as a precautionary control per Simplicit's compliance program.

**Action:** Amendments being prepared; execution targeted August 31, 2026.

### Finding 2 — Visual Compliance Screening Not Triggered for One Spot Purchase
In April 2026, a one-time spot purchase of cutting tool consumables ($1,200) from a new vendor was processed without triggering the Visual Compliance screening workflow due to a purchasing system bypass. The vendor was subsequently screened and cleared; no violation occurred.

**Action:** Procurement procedure updated to require screening certificate before any new vendor invoice is approved. IT applied system-level control in June.
