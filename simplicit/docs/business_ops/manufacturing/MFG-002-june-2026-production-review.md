## 1. Executive Summary

June 2026 production performance was mixed. Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) recovered marginally from May lows (78.6%) but remained at 79.1% for the month — still below the 82% target. The Line 2 scrap corrective action is showing early progress (scrap rate 3.6% in June vs. 4.8% in May) but recovery is not yet complete. Throughput on Lines 1, 3, and 4 was solid, largely offsetting Line 2 underperformance. On-Time Delivery (OTD) reached 99.7% — a highlight — driven by proactive scheduling adjustments by the Plant A team.

## 4. Downtime Pareto — June 2026

Total downtime recorded: 412 hours across all lines (planned + unplanned).

| Downtime Category | Hours | % of Total | Line Primarily Affected |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unplanned — Equipment Failure** | 127 | 30.8% | L2 (CNC 2-C bearing), L1 (coolant pump) |
| **Planned — PM / Setup** | 118 | 28.6% | All lines |
| **Quality Hold / Inspection** | 98 | 23.8% | L2 (100% incoming inspection) |
| **Material Shortage / Staging** | 42 | 10.2% | L3 (Harbor Steel delivery delay) |
| **Operator Absenteeism** | 27 | 6.6% | Various |
| **Total** | **412** | 100% | |

**Top Equipment Events:**
1. **CNC Cell 2-C — Spindle Bearing Failure (June 12):** 48-hour unplanned downtime. Machine was scheduled for bearing inspection in Q3 PM cycle; failure occurred early. Root cause: bearing failed within service life, possibly related to elevated cutting forces from harder-than-nominal titanium material (see MFG-001). Replacement completed June 14. Maintenance is reviewing whether similar proactive bearing replacements are warranted on L2 CNC Cell 2-A and 2-D.
2. **Line 1 — Coolant Pump Failure (June 21):** 18-hour unplanned downtime. Pump replaced from on-hand spare. PM interval for coolant system will be shortened from 12 months to 8 months.
3. **Quality Hold / Incoming Inspection (L2):** The 100% incoming inspection requirement (containment action from MFG-001) adds approximately 2 hours of hold time per titanium lot before release to line. 49 lots inspected in June, generating 98 hours of effective downtime. This is a planned cost of the containment action and will reduce as the real-time SPC system (CAP-004) comes online.

## 6. On-Time Delivery Performance

**June OTD: 99.7%** — Best month in the past 18 months.

This result reflects deliberate scheduling adjustments made in late May after the April/May OTD softness. Plant A Scheduling Coordinator George Young revised the Line 2 scheduling algorithm to build 3-day buffer into Aerospace orders given the scrap uncertainty. This buffer cushioned the impact of Line 2 availability issues and allowed all customer commitments to be met.

**Customer-specific OTD:**
- Cascade Defense Systems (CUS003): 100% — all 14 June shipments on time
- Bolt Dynamics (CUS002): 99% — 1 shipment delayed 1 day due to final inspection queue
- Eagle Components (CUS005): 100%
- Northstar Aerospace (CUS014): 100%

## 8. H2 2026 Production Priorities

Based on the June review, the top three manufacturing priorities for H2 2026 are:

1. **Line 2 scrap recovery to ≤2.5% by September 30** — Operational and financial imperative.
2. **Throughput capacity for H2 demand:** VP Sales forecasts Q3 bookings recovery requiring approximately 5% volume uplift from Q2 actuals. Currently feasible if Line 2 recovers; overtime authorization may be needed in August–September.
3. **Workforce stabilization:** 17 open Manufacturing requisitions must be filled to support H2 production plan. Kimberly Gonzalez has requested HR priority support.
