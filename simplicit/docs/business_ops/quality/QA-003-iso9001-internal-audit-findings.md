## 2. Major Nonconformances

### MNC-01 — Document Control (ISO 9001:2015 §7.5)
**Finding:** Fourteen controlled documents found in use across three departments (Manufacturing, Engineering, Quality) were not at their current approved revision. Specifically:
- Work Instruction WI-MFG-017 (Lathe Setup — Line 2): Rev C in use; Rev E approved April 2, 2026
- Engineering Drawing 4412-B-003: Rev 7 posted on shop floor; Rev 9 is current per ECR log
- 12 additional SOPs in the Engineering change log had superseded versions still posted at workstations

**Objective Evidence:** Physical copies at workstations; comparison against Master Document List (MDL-2026-Q1) confirmed discrepancy.

**Risk:** Operators following obsolete work instructions may produce nonconforming product. The P/N 4412-B dimensional issue (CC-2026-023 at Hartwell) may be partially attributable to operators using Rev 7 drawing.

**Required Action:** CAPA-2026-010 opened. Immediate containment: retrieve all obsolete documents and replace with current revisions within 5 business days. Systemic fix: implement document distribution control (electronic or stamped "CONTROLLED COPY") and add MDL audit to monthly Quality checklist.

**Owner:** Sandra Okonkwo | **Due:** August 15, 2026

---

### MNC-02 — Monitoring and Measuring Resources — Calibration Records (ISO 9001:2015 §7.1.5)
**Finding:** Calibration records for 23 of 148 measurement instruments were overdue as of audit date. Instruments affected include:
- 6 micrometers (Plant 1 Line 2) — overdue 14–62 days
- 4 bore gauges (Quality Inspection) — overdue 7–31 days
- 8 torque wrenches (Assembly) — overdue 3–45 days
- 5 temperature probes (Heat Treat area) — overdue 21–89 days

**Objective Evidence:** Calibration log (CAL-MASTER-2026) cross-referenced with physical instrument tags.

**Risk:** Measurements taken with out-of-calibration instruments may be invalid. Results recorded with these instruments during the overdue period require suspect product review.

**Required Action:** CAPA-2026-011 opened. Immediate: quarantine all 23 instruments pending calibration; conduct suspect product review for parts measured exclusively with overdue instruments during the overdue window. Systemic fix: automate calibration due-date alerts in the CMMS system; assign dedicated calibration technician responsibility.

**Owner:** Kevin Park | **Due:** August 15, 2026

## 3. Minor Nonconformances

### MNC-03 — Competence Records (§7.2)
**Finding:** Training records for 11 employees (primarily recent hires Q1 2026) were incomplete — job-specific competency sign-offs missing for 3 required tasks per employee.
**Corrective Action:** HR to complete training record audit and close gaps by June 30, 2026.
**Status:** In progress (HR-003 cross-reference)

### MNC-04 — Customer Communication — Complaint Tracking (§8.2.1)
**Finding:** Customer complaint CC-2026-018 was not formally entered into the NCR system until 18 days after receipt. The complaint handling procedure (PROC-QA-005) requires entry within 2 business days.
**Corrective Action:** Retrain Sales and Quality staff on complaint intake procedure; add complaint receipt to daily Quality morning review.

### MNC-05 — Supplier Evaluation Records (§8.4.1)
**Finding:** Annual supplier re-evaluation not completed for 4 of 22 active approved suppliers. Evaluations were due Q4 2025 and remain incomplete as of audit.
**Corrective Action:** Complete all overdue evaluations by June 30, 2026; add to annual Quality calendar.
**Status:** Complete (all 4 evaluations finished June 20, 2026)

### MNC-06 — Internal Audit Program (§9.2)
**Finding:** Internal audit of the IT and Legal departments was not completed in FY2025 as required by the annual audit schedule (QP-QA-003). No documented reason for the omission.
**Corrective Action:** Schedule and complete IT and Legal audits by September 30, 2026; revise audit scheduling to include completion tracking and management review.

### MNC-07 — Management Review Inputs (§9.3.2)
**Finding:** The Q4 2025 Management Review meeting minutes did not include discussion of "performance of external providers" as required by §9.3.2(d). The agenda item was listed but the minutes show it was deferred and not rescheduled.
**Corrective Action:** Add supplier performance to Q2 2026 QMR agenda (addressed — see QA-001); update management review agenda template to flag required inputs.
