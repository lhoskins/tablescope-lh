## 3. Deal-Level Analysis

### 3a. Slipped Deals (Q2 → Q3)

| Deal | Customer | Value | Slip Reason | Q3 Close Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Automotive Shaft Set — 2H PO | Bolt Dynamics (CUS002) | $480K | Customer production cycle change; Q3 confirmed in writing | August 2026 |
| Industrial Connector Assembly | Eagle Components (CUS005) | $390K | Customer ERP integration delay; decision maker unreachable in June | July 2026 |
| Medical Precision Rod | Delta Precision Works (CUS004) | $340K | Customer FDA timeline pushed; product launch Q4 2026 | Q4 2026 |

**Assessment:** All three slipped deals remain in the pipeline and are considered qualified. The Bolt Dynamics and Eagle Components deals are expected to close in Q3. The Delta Precision Works deal has slipped to Q4, creating a meaningful timing gap in the medical segment.

### 3b. Competitive Losses

| Deal | Customer | Value Lost | Competitor | Loss Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Precision Shaft — 3 SKUs | Greenfield Industries (CUS007) | $380K | Midlands Machining (Cincinnati) | 8% price gap on commodity geometry |
| Custom Bearing Housing | Quantum Precision (CUS017) | $140K | Regional shop (unidentified) | Lead time; Quantum needed 2-week delivery; Simplicit quoted 4 weeks |

**Greenfield Industries loss analysis:**
The Greenfield loss is the more significant competitive setback. These are not premium engineered parts — they are medium-complexity precision shafts where Greenfield uses competitive bidding. Midlands Machining undercut Simplicit by approximately 8% on unit price. When Karen Sanchez (Sales Manager) met with the Greenfield procurement team in May, the feedback was: "We like Simplicit for complex parts. For catalog-type work, you're just not price competitive."

Margaret Green and Kimberly Martinez discussed whether Simplicit should participate in commodity bidding for accounts like Greenfield, or focus the account relationship exclusively on complex, engineered parts where the company has a defensible margin position. Decision: Pursue a "tiered portfolio" approach with Greenfield — concede low-margin commodity work but pursue two new complex programs (5-axis machined housing, tolerance stack assembly) in Q3. Greenfield account plan updated accordingly.

**Quantum Precision loss analysis:**
The Quantum loss was an operational execution failure — a 4-week lead time quote when the customer needed 2 weeks. The pricing was competitive; the lead time was not. This order should have been identified as a rush requirement in the needs-discovery phase. Sales process gap: lead time sensitivity is not consistently surfaced in the initial qualification conversation.

**Action:** Anthony Harris (Sales Manager) will implement a lead time screening question as a mandatory step in the discovery qualification checklist by July 31.
