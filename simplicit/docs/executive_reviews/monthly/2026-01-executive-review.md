## Key Issues and Actions

### 1. Voluntary Turnover Spike (Red)
**Situation:** Three Engineering and two Manufacturing employees submitted resignations in January, bringing the annualized voluntary turnover rate to 14.2% vs. 8% target. Exit interviews indicate compensation competitiveness and workload as primary drivers.  
**Action:** CHRO to present retention analysis and proposed actions to CEO by February 14. Compensation benchmarking initiated. Two immediate counter-offer cases authorized by CEO.  
**Owner:** CHRO | **Due:** February 14

### 2. On-Time Delivery (Yellow)
**Situation:** Two customer shipments delayed due to material shortages (titanium hex bar). Customers notified proactively.  
**Action:** Procurement to identify backup supplier. Engineering to review BOM for any substitution opportunities.  
**Owner:** Procurement Director | **Due:** February 15
