## Executive Summary

February brought two significant developments. First, material costs exceeded budget by 9.2% — the first month of what appears to be a systemic trend as raw material prices rose. Finance has flagged this as an emerging risk to full-year margins. Second, voluntary turnover continued at an elevated rate for the second consecutive month. The CHRO's retention proposal is under review. On a positive note, OEE improved on three of four production lines, and bookings tracked ahead of plan.

## Material Cost Variance Deep Dive

Finance completed an analysis of the February material cost overrun:
- **Titanium alloy:** Price increased 11.4% from Q4 2025 supplier pricing
- **Steel rod stock:** Price increased 7.8% from prior contract rate (contract expired January 31)
- **Fasteners:** Flat; contract locked through June 2026

**Projected full-year impact if trend continues:** ~$1.4M in unbudgeted material costs, or approximately 3.3 margin points on a full-year basis.

**Actions:**
1. Procurement Director to complete contract renegotiations with top 3 material suppliers by March 31
2. CFO to update full-year forecast and present to CEO
3. VP Sales to assess whether selective price increases are feasible on renewal orders
