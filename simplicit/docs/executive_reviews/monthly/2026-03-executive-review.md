## Executive Summary

March marks the close of Q1 2026. The quarter ended with revenue slightly ahead of plan but meaningful headwinds in gross margin driven by material cost inflation. A new operational concern emerged: the Line 2 scrap rate at Plant A began trending upward in the second half of March, reaching 3.4% — well above the 2.5% target. Engineering and Quality are investigating. Turnover stabilized somewhat, though we remain above target.

## Line 2 Scrap Rate — Emerging Issue

**Observation:** Line 2 scrap rate increased from a baseline of ~2.2% in January-February to 3.4% in March. The increase appeared mid-March and has been consistent across multiple part numbers.  
**Potential Causes under investigation:** Tooling wear (cutting inserts replaced last in December), coolant system issue (maintenance PM due April), raw material lot variability.  
**Actions:**  
1. Quality and Engineering conducting cause mapping exercise — complete April 10
2. Maintenance accelerating Line 2 PM to April 14
3. Quality placing enhanced inspection at Line 2 (100% dimensional check on affected part numbers) effective immediately

**Owner:** VP Manufacturing + VP Quality | **Due:** April 14 (root cause report)
