## Executive Summary

April presented a complex picture. The Line 2 scrap investigation concluded with a preliminary root cause (coolant system contamination and tooling wear) but the fix is taking longer than expected — scrap reached 4.1% by month end. Q2 bookings came in 16% below plan, the first month of what appears to be a broader softening of demand. Material costs continued to run approximately 10% over budget. These three concurrent headwinds — margin pressure, operational scrap, and booking slippage — require coordinated executive action.
