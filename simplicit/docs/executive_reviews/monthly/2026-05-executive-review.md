The May 7 Emergency Operations Review produced a prioritized action plan that is now in execution. The team is focused.

## Line 2 Scrap — Progress Update

The Line 2 scrap rate declined from 4.1% in April to 3.6% in May following:
- Coolant system full drain and replacement (completed April 28)
- Tooling insert specification upgraded from standard to premium grade (Engineering ECR issued April 30)
- Operator SPC training completed for Line 2 team (Quality, May 10)

Engineering projects full recovery to <2.5% by mid-June as the new tooling establishes steady-state performance. Weekly scrap tracking continues.

## Bookings and Pipeline — VP Sales Update

VP Sales presented root cause analysis to CEO May 10:
- **Apex Aerospace (RSK-008):** $2.1M in anticipated H1 orders delayed to H2 due to customer program schedule shift. Contract relationship intact; VP Sales met with Apex VP Procurement May 19.
- **General market softness:** Two Automotive customers reduced order volumes, citing inventory destocking.

**Recovery actions:**
- 2 new Sales Representatives started May 12 (Midwest territory)
- BD identified 4 new aerospace prospects; first meetings scheduled June
- VP Sales targeting $4.8M bookings in June to partially offset Q2 shortfall
