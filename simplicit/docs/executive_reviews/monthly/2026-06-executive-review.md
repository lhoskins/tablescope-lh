## Executive Summary

June closed out a difficult Q2. Bookings finished 15.2% below plan for the quarter — in line with our updated forecast. The Line 2 scrap rate improved to 3.1% but remains above target; Engineering expects full recovery in July with the new tooling now fully settled. A second wave of voluntary departures hit in June — four Manufacturing technicians and two Sales staff — largely driven by retention bonus offers from a regional competitor. The engineering cost overrun on projects EPRJ-003 and EPRJ-007 is confirmed at 22% and 28% respectively, requiring CEO attention on project scope and funding. H2 starts with a clear action plan and improving operational trajectory.

## June Turnover — Second Wave

Four Manufacturing technicians and two Sales staff departed in June. Exit interviews revealed a regional competitor is actively poaching with 12–18% salary premiums.

**Actions:**
- CHRO accelerating off-cycle compensation review (original timeline: Q4) to complete by August 1
- Immediate job posting for all 6 positions; 2 already in process
- CEO personally contacted 3 "at risk" employees identified by managers

## H2 2026 Outlook

CFO updated full-year forecast:
- **Revenue:** $45.2M (vs. $47.0M plan) — 3.8% below plan
- **Gross Margin %:** 35.8% (vs. 38.0% plan) — pressure from material costs and scrap
- **EBITDA %:** 12.4% (vs. 14.0% plan)

H2 recovery drivers:
1. Material cost savings (~$65K/month from renegotiated contracts)
2. Line 2 scrap recovery to target
3. Apex Aerospace orders expected to ramp in Q3
4. New Sales reps contributing to bookings pipeline by Q3
