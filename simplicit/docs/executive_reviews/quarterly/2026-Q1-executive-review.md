# Q1 2026 Quarterly Executive Review

**Period:** Q1 2026 (January – March 2026)  
**Prepared by:** CFO / COO  
**Distributed:** April 10, 2026  
**Audience:** Executive Team, Board of Directors  
**Classification:** Internal — Confidential  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Q1 2026 closed with revenue modestly ahead of plan, supported by strong bookings in January and February. However, the quarter exposed two significant emerging risks that will define our H1 priorities: (1) material cost inflation that compressed gross margins by 130 basis points relative to plan, and (2) voluntary turnover in Engineering and Manufacturing that reached a three-year high. On balance, the business is growing, our customers remain satisfied, and our operational fundamentals are sound — but disciplined action on costs and talent is required to protect full-year results.

---

## 9. Board-Level Narrative

The company enters Q2 from a position of growth — revenue is up 7% year-over-year and customer satisfaction is at a multi-year high. The challenges we face are operational and cost-driven, not market-driven. Our top three priorities for the leadership team through H1 are:

1. **Protect the margin:** Renegotiate material contracts, investigate Line 2 scrap root cause, and evaluate selective customer price adjustments.
2. **Win the talent battle:** Execute the retention program, complete compensation benchmarking, and improve hiring velocity.
3. **Deliver the strategic investments:** ERP modernization vendor selection, CNC installation, and aerospace new product development are all on or near schedule.

The company's fundamentals — customer relationships, quality reputation, and operational capability — remain strong. The next 90 days will determine whether H2 can recover the margin gap.
