## 1. Executive Summary

Q2 2026 was the most challenging quarter Simplicit Demo Company has faced in three years. Three simultaneous headwinds — sustained material cost inflation, the Line 2 scrap trend, and a meaningful shortfall in bookings — compressed EBITDA to 12.1% against a 14.0% target. Revenue declined relative to plan for the first time since Q3 2023. The positive narrative: we have root-caused all three issues, corrective actions are underway and showing early results, the customer relationship base is intact, and the balance sheet remains healthy. H2 execution will determine whether we recover to a full-year near-plan outcome or report a meaningful miss.

## 3. Material Cost Variance — Root Cause and Mitigation

The primary margin driver in H1 was raw material price inflation:

| Material | Budget Rate (Q1 start) | Peak Actual Rate (May) | Renegotiated Rate (Jun+) | Savings/Month |
|----------|------------------------|------------------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| Titanium alloy bar | $48.20/lb | $53.85/lb | $51.40/lb | ~$28K/mo |

New contracts effective June 1 will reduce the overrun from ~$190K/month to ~$100K/month — partial relief, but not a full reset. Procurement is evaluating long-term fixed-price contracts for 2027 budget cycle.

## 4. Line 2 Scrap — Investigation Findings and Recovery

**Root Cause (confirmed April):** Two contributing causes:
1. Coolant system contamination (bacterial growth in coolant sump not caught in routine PM) that degraded surface finish quality, causing dimensional scatter
2. Cutting insert specification had not been updated after a December 2025 material grade change; the old insert grade wore faster, increasing scrap

**Actions Taken:**
- Coolant system fully drained, cleaned, and recharged (April 28)
- Engineering ECR issued new cutting insert specification (April 30, implemented May 5)
- SPC training for Line 2 operators completed (May 10)
- PM interval for coolant system reduced from 90 days to 45 days (effective May)

**Recovery Trajectory:**

| Month | Line 2 Scrap Rate | Status |
|-------|------------------|--------|
| February (pre-issue) | 2.2% | Baseline |
| March | 3.4% | Issue identified |
| April | 4.1% | Peak |
| May | 3.6% | Improving |
| June | 3.1% | Further improving |
| July (week 1) | 2.9% | Near target |

Engineering projects sustained <2.5% by end of July.

### Full-Year Updated Forecast

| Metric | Original Plan | Revised Forecast | Gap |

| Revenue | $47.0M | $45.2M | -$1.8M (-3.8%) |
| Gross Margin % | 38.0% | 35.8% | -220 bps |

### H2 Recovery Levers

1. **Material cost savings** ($65K/month × 6 months = $390K)
2. **Line 2 scrap normalization** (~$45K/month when at target vs. current)
3. **Apex Aerospace ramp** (~$1.8M in delayed bookings expected Q3)
4. **New rep productivity** ($400K+ incremental bookings projected Q3-Q4)
5. **Headcount recovery** (24 open positions; cost savings on unrealized salary until filled estimated at $185K H2 — but also limits production capacity)

### H2 Risk: If Automotive Demand Doesn't Recover
If automotive segment remains at Q2 run rate through H2, the revenue gap widens by ~$900K and full-year EBITDA falls to ~$5.1M (11.0%). This is a scenario we are managing actively, not expecting.

## 12. Board-Level Narrative

Q2 was hard. Three simultaneous challenges hit the business — none of which were foreseeable at the start of the year — and the cumulative impact on margins was significant. The leadership team's response has been focused and disciplined.

What gives us confidence in H2:
- The root causes are understood and being corrected
- Customer relationships are intact — no lost accounts, no material NPS decline
- The balance sheet has capacity and covenant headroom
- Pipeline coverage is 3.1x, with visible upside from Apex Aerospace ramp
- Two corrective levers (material contracts, Line 2 recovery) are already in motion

The Board should hold management accountable for the following milestones through Q3 reporting:
1. Line 2 scrap sustained at ≤ 2.5% for 4+ weeks
2. Q3 bookings > $13M (recovering from Q2)
3. Compensation review completed and retention risk reduced
4. Gross margin recovery to ≥ 36.5% in Q3
