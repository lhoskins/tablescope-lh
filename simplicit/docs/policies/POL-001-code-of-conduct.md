# POL-001: Code of Conduct

**Policy ID:** POL-001  
**Title:** Code of Conduct  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Chief Human Resources Officer  
**Scope:** All employees, contractors, and third-party representatives of Simplicit Demo Company  
**Next Review Date:** January 1, 2027  

---

## 1. Purpose

Simplicit Demo Company is committed to maintaining the highest standards of ethical conduct in all business activities. This Code of Conduct establishes the behavioral standards expected of every person who represents the company, ensuring that our actions reflect our values of integrity, respect, accountability, and excellence.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees, part-time employees, temporary workers, contractors, consultants, and any other individuals acting on behalf of Simplicit Demo Company, regardless of location or job function.

---

## 3. Core Values and Expected Behaviors

### 3.1 Integrity
Employees must be honest in all business dealings — with customers, suppliers, regulators, and one another. No employee may falsify records, misrepresent facts, or engage in deceptive practices of any kind.

### 3.2 Respect and Inclusion
Every individual in our workplace deserves to be treated with dignity and respect. Discrimination, harassment, or hostile behavior based on race, gender, religion, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic is strictly prohibited. (See also POL-007: Anti-Harassment.)

### 3.3 Accountability
Employees are responsible for their own actions and decisions. Mistakes should be acknowledged promptly and corrective action taken. Blame-shifting, concealment of errors, or retaliatory behavior is contrary to our culture.

### 3.4 Compliance with Laws and Regulations
All employees must comply with applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations, including those governing manufacturing safety (OSHA), environmental protection (EPA), export controls (EAR/ITAR), and financial reporting.

### 3.5 Protection of Company Assets
Company assets — physical, financial, and intellectual — must be used responsibly and only for legitimate business purposes. Theft, misuse, or unauthorized disclosure of company assets is prohibited.

---

## 4. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict — or could appear to conflict — with the interests of Simplicit Demo Company. Examples include:

- Holding a significant financial interest in a competitor, customer, or supplier
- Accepting gifts, meals, or entertainment from suppliers valued above $50 per occurrence or $150 per calendar year
- Hiring or directly managing a close family member without prior HR approval
- Using company resources or confidential information for personal financial gain

All potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed in writing to your manager and HR within 30 days of the potential conflict arising.

---

## 5. Confidentiality

Employees may have access to sensitive business information including customer data, pricing, formulas, designs, and financial results. This information must not be shared outside the company except as required by law or as authorized in writing. Obligations continue after employment ends. (See also POL-008: Confidentiality and NDA.)

---

## 6. Use of Company Resources

Company equipment, systems, software, and facilities are provided for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work responsibilities, consume significant resources, or violate other policies. (See also POL-003: Acceptable Use of IT Resources.)

---

## 7. Political and Charitable Activities

Simplicit Demo Company does not make corporate contributions to political campaigns. Employees may engage in personal political activity on their own time using personal resources, but must not imply company endorsement or use company assets for political purposes. Charitable activities using company resources require prior management approval.

---

## 8. Reporting Violations — Speaking Up

Employees who observe or suspect violations of this Code or any company policy are expected to report them. Reports may be made to:

- Your direct manager
- Human Resources
- The Company Ethics Hotline (anonymous): 1-800-SDC-ETHX (available 24/7)
- Legal Counsel

Simplicit Demo Company prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith. Retaliation is itself a violation of this Code subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination.

---

## 9. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| All Employees | Read, understand, and comply with this Code; complete annual certification |
| Managers | Model expected behaviors; address and report observed violations promptly |
| HR | Maintain this policy; manage investigations; administer training |
| Legal | Advise on compliance; oversee investigations when warranted |
| Executive Team | Champion ethical culture; review Code annually; approve revisions |

---

## 10. Compliance and Enforcement

Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. In some cases, violations may also result in civil or criminal liability. The severity of the response will be proportionate to the nature of the violation, the employee's history, and any mitigating or aggravating factors.

Annual certification of this Code is required for all employees. New employees must complete certification within 30 days of hire as part of onboarding.
