# POL-004: AI Acceptable Use Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-004  
**Title:** Artificial Intelligence Acceptable Use Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Chief Information Officer / Chief Operating Officer  
**Scope:** All employees using AI tools on behalf of Simplicit Demo Company  
**Next Review Date:** January 1, 2027  

---

## 1. Purpose

Artificial intelligence tools — including large language models, generative AI systems, AI-assisted design tools, and AI-based analytics — offer significant productivity potential. This policy establishes guardrails to ensure AI tools are used responsibly, legally, and in ways that protect the company, its customers, and its employees.

---

## 2. Approved AI Tools

Only AI tools that have been reviewed and approved by IT and Legal may be used for company work. The current approved list is maintained on the IT Intranet page. Employees may request evaluation of new AI tools by submitting a request to the IT Help Desk with the business justification.

Using unapproved AI tools for company work is prohibited, as these tools may not provide adequate data protection and their outputs may carry licensing risks.

---

## 3. What You May Use AI For

Approved AI tools may be used for:
- Drafting and editing internal documents, reports, and communications
- Summarizing publicly available information for research purposes
- Generating code for internal use (with appropriate human review)
- Brainstorming, ideation, and outlining
- Translation of business documents
- Analyzing data that has been appropriately de-identified
- Preparing presentations and training materials

---

## 4. Prohibited Uses of AI

### 4.1 Data Confidentiality
Employees must NOT input the following into any AI tool unless specifically cleared by IT and Legal:
- Customer names, contact information, or business data
- Employee personal information (names, salaries, performance data)
- Proprietary formulas, designs, drawings, or trade secrets
- ITAR/EAR-controlled technical data
- Financial data not yet publicly disclosed
- Ongoing legal matters or attorney-client privileged content
- Any Restricted-classified information (per POL-002)

**Note:** Content entered into AI tools may be used to train or improve those models unless enterprise data-protection agreements are in place. Assume inputs are not private unless IT confirms otherwise.

### 4.2 Accuracy and Reliability
AI tools can produce plausible-sounding but incorrect information ("hallucinations"). Employees must:
- Independently verify factual claims before relying on AI-generated content in business decisions
- Not use AI output as the sole basis for regulatory compliance, legal advice, engineering specifications, or financial reporting
- Clearly label AI-assisted work as such when sharing with customers or external parties, unless content has been thoroughly reviewed and verified

### 4.3 Bias and Ethics
- AI tools must not be used to make or influence employment decisions (hiring, termination, promotion) without explicit HR and Legal review
- AI-generated content must be reviewed to ensure it does not contain discriminatory, misleading, or offensive material

---

## 5. Intellectual Property

AI-generated content may carry copyright ambiguity. Employees should not reproduce substantial portions of AI-generated text verbatim in customer-facing materials, patents, or published works without Legal review. Verify that AI tools do not incorporate third-party copyrighted material into outputs used externally.

---

## 6. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| All Employees | Use only approved AI tools; protect confidential data; verify outputs |
| IT | Maintain approved tool list; evaluate new tools; ensure enterprise data agreements |
| Legal | Review IP and compliance risks; advise on data protection for AI tools |
| Managers | Ensure team members understand and follow this policy |
| CISO | Monitor AI tool usage for security risks; incident response |

---

## 7. Training

All employees using AI tools for company work must complete AI Responsible Use training within 60 days of this policy's effective date, and annually thereafter.

---

## 8. Compliance and Enforcement

Violations may result in revocation of AI tool access, disciplinary action, and potential legal liability if confidential data is exposed. Suspected violations must be reported to IT and HR.
