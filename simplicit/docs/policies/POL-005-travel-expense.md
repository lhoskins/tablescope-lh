# POL-005: Travel and Expense Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-005  
**Title:** Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Chief Financial Officer  
**Scope:** All employees traveling on company business or incurring business expenses  
**Next Review Date:** January 1, 2027  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy provides guidelines for booking business travel, incurring business expenses, and seeking reimbursement. It balances the need to manage company costs prudently while enabling employees to travel and work effectively.

---

## 2. Pre-Approval Requirements

Travel requiring air travel or overnight stays must be pre-approved by the employee's manager before booking. Trips with total estimated cost exceeding $2,500 require VP-level approval. International travel requires VP and CFO approval.

---

## 3. Booking Requirements

All airline and hotel bookings must be made through the company's designated travel management platform (currently Concur). Direct bookings are permitted only when the platform does not offer an equivalent option; all direct bookings require manager approval and an explanation upon expense submission.

### 3.1 Air Travel
- Book economy class for domestic flights under 6 hours
- Business class is permitted for international flights over 6 hours or with VP+ approval
- Book at least 14 days in advance when possible
- Choose itineraries within 20% of the lowest logical fare

### 3.2 Hotels
- Use company-preferred hotel program when available
- Maximum nightly rate: $225 (Tier 1 cities: NYC, SF, Chicago, Boston); $175 (other cities)
- Exceptions require manager pre-approval

### 3.3 Ground Transportation
- Use rideshare (Uber, Lyft) or public transit when economical
- Rental cars require manager approval; select mid-size or smaller unless business need requires otherwise
- Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate

---

## 4. Expense Limits and Guidelines

| Expense Category | Guideline |
|-----------------|-----------|
| Meals (solo) | $60/day domestic; $80/day international |
| Business meals (with clients) | $100/person; itemized receipt required; list all attendees |
| Alcohol | Reimbursable as part of client entertainment (with VP approval); not reimbursable as solo expense |
| Tips | Up to 20% on meals and transportation |
| Internet/Phone (travel) | Actual cost; no per-diem limit |
| Incidentals | $25/day (parking, tolls, checked baggage) |
| Gifts to clients | Max $50/recipient/year; VP approval required above $25 |

---

## 5. Expense Reporting and Receipts

- Submit expense reports within 30 days of expense incurrence or return from travel
- Receipts are required for all expenses over $25
- Credit card statements alone do not satisfy receipt requirements
- Reports must be approved by the employee's manager before reimbursement processing

Expenses submitted more than 90 days after incurrence will not be reimbursed without CFO approval.

---

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursable under any circumstances:
- Personal entertainment, movies, or minibar charges
- Traffic citations, parking fines, or vehicle towing
- Travel insurance not booked through the corporate travel platform
- Expenses for personal travel days added to business trips (beyond the prorated incremental cost)
- Luxury upgrades (suites, first-class domestic, etc.) without pre-approval
- Expenses for spouses, partners, or family members

---

## 7. Corporate Cards

Employees with company travel cards must use them for all business travel and entertainment expenses. Personal expenses on corporate cards are prohibited. Failure to pay the balance through timely expense reporting may result in personal liability.

---

## 8. Compliance and Enforcement

Finance conducts periodic audits of expense reports. Employees found to have submitted fraudulent or non-compliant expenses will be required to repay the amount and may be subject to disciplinary action up to termination.
