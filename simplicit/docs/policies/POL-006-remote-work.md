# POL-006: Remote Work Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-006  
**Title:** Remote Work Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Chief Human Resources Officer  
**Scope:** Eligible salaried employees of Simplicit Demo Company  
**Next Review Date:** January 1, 2027  

---

## 1. Purpose

Simplicit Demo Company supports flexible work arrangements where operationally feasible. This policy establishes the conditions under which remote work is permitted, to maintain productivity, collaboration, security, and fairness across our workforce.

---

## 2. Eligibility

Remote work is available to salaried employees in roles that can be performed fully from a remote location without material reduction in effectiveness. Due to the nature of our manufacturing operations, the following roles are generally ineligible for full remote work:

- Production line operators and technicians
- Quality inspection and lab personnel
- Facilities and maintenance staff
- EHS field personnel

Managers and HR will make eligibility determinations for roles in a gray area on a case-by-case basis.

---

## 3. Remote Work Arrangements

### 3.1 Hybrid (Standard)
Most eligible roles default to a hybrid arrangement: a minimum of 2 days per week in the office or plant, with the remaining days remote. Specific in-office days are determined by department and manager.

### 3.2 Fully Remote
Fully remote arrangements are available for specific roles or exceptional circumstances, subject to VP and HR approval. Fully remote employees must be available for quarterly in-person attendance at the Columbus HQ.

### 3.3 Temporary Remote Work
Employees may request temporary remote work (up to 30 consecutive days) for personal circumstances (e.g., family care) with manager approval. Temporary arrangements may not become indefinite without following the formal arrangement process.

---

## 4. Expectations for Remote Employees

### 4.1 Availability
Remote employees must maintain the same availability and response times as they would in the office during their designated working hours. Core hours for all employees are 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM ET.

### 4.2 Communication
- Keep your calendar current and status updated in Teams
- Respond to email and messages within 2 hours during core hours
- Attend all video meetings with camera on unless bandwidth or privacy constraints prevent it

### 4.3 Work Environment
- Maintain a dedicated, professional workspace with adequate internet connectivity (minimum 25 Mbps upload/download)
- Ensure confidential discussions cannot be overheard in shared spaces
- The company will reimburse up to $500 one-time for home office equipment (pre-approved by manager)

---

## 5. Security Requirements

Remote employees must comply with all requirements in POL-002 (Information Security). Key requirements:

- Use the company VPN whenever accessing internal systems
- Lock screens when stepping away
- Do not conduct company business on personal devices unless enrolled in MDM
- Do not work from public Wi-Fi without VPN

---

## 6. Performance Standards

Remote work is a privilege contingent on performance. Managers may require a return to on-site work if performance issues arise or business needs change. Remote work arrangements are not guaranteed and may be modified with 30 days' notice.

---

## 7. Compensation and Benefits

Remote work arrangements do not affect compensation or benefits eligibility. Employees who relocate must notify HR; relocation to a different state may affect compensation, tax withholding, and benefit eligibility and requires VP and HR approval.
