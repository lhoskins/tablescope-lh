# POL-007: Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-007  
**Title:** Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Chief Human Resources Officer  
**Scope:** All employees, contractors, vendors, and visitors at Simplicit Demo Company  

---

## 1. Purpose

Simplicit Demo Company is committed to providing a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and hostile behavior. Every employee has the right to work in an environment that is respectful, professional, and free from intimidation.

---

## 2. Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is prohibited and will not be tolerated:

### 2.1 Harassment
Unwelcome conduct based on any protected characteristic (see 2.3) that is severe or pervasive enough to create a hostile, intimidating, or abusive work environment. Examples include offensive jokes, slurs, threats, mockery, or physical interference.

### 2.2 Sexual Harassment
Sexual harassment includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature when:
- Submission is made explicitly or implicitly a term of employment
- Submission or rejection affects employment decisions
- Such conduct creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment

### 2.3 Discrimination
Adverse employment decisions (hiring, promotion, compensation, discipline, termination) based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age (40+), disability, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic.

---

## 3. Reporting

Employees who experience or witness harassment or discrimination should report it as soon as possible through any of the following:
- Their manager (if not involved in the situation)
- HR Director or any HR Business Partner
- Ethics Hotline: 1-800-SDC-ETHX (anonymous)
- Legal Counsel

Employees are not required to confront the offending party before reporting.

---

## 4. Investigation Process

HR will acknowledge reports within 2 business days. Investigations will be:
- Conducted promptly and thoroughly
- Completed by trained HR investigators or external investigators when appropriate
- Kept as confidential as practicable (complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed)
- Free from retaliation for all participants

Findings will be communicated to the reporting party in general terms consistent with confidentiality obligations.

---

## 5. Non-Retaliation

Retaliation against any person who reports harassment or discrimination in good faith, participates in an investigation, or files a charge with a government agency is strictly prohibited. Retaliation is subject to discipline up to and including termination.

---

## 6. Consequences

Substantiated violations will result in disciplinary action commensurate with severity, up to and including termination. Particularly egregious conduct may be referred to law enforcement.
