# POL-008: Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-008  
**Title:** Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** General Counsel  
**Scope:** All employees, contractors, and third parties with access to confidential Simplicit Demo Company information  

---

## 1. Purpose

Simplicit Demo Company's competitive advantage depends on protecting proprietary business information. This policy defines obligations with respect to confidential and proprietary information.

---

## 2. Confidential Information Defined

"Confidential Information" includes, without limitation:
- Business strategies, plans, and forecasts
- Customer lists, pricing, and contract terms
- Supplier relationships and pricing
- Product designs, engineering drawings, formulas, and specifications
- Financial results not publicly disclosed
- Software code and algorithms
- Personnel data and compensation
- Any information designated "Confidential" or "Restricted" under POL-002

---

## 3. Employee Obligations

All employees must:
- Access Confidential Information only to the extent necessary for their role
- Not disclose Confidential Information to unauthorized persons (including other employees without a need to know)
- Not use Confidential Information for personal benefit
- Return or destroy Confidential Information upon request or employment separation
- Comply with all NDAs and confidentiality agreements signed in connection with employment

Obligations survive the termination of employment indefinitely for trade secrets, and for a period of three (3) years for other Confidential Information.

---

## 4. Third-Party NDAs

Before sharing Confidential Information with a third party (vendor, partner, customer, prospective customer), a fully executed Non-Disclosure Agreement must be in place. Template NDAs are available from Legal. All NDAs must be reviewed and approved by Legal before execution.

---

## 5. Inadvertent Disclosure

If you suspect Confidential Information has been disclosed without authorization, notify Legal and your manager immediately. Prompt reporting minimizes harm and allows the company to take protective action.
