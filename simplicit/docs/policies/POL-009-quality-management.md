# POL-009: Quality Management Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-009  
**Title:** Quality Management System Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Vice President, Quality  
**Scope:** All operations, products, and processes of Simplicit Demo Company  

---

## 1. Purpose

Simplicit Demo Company is committed to delivering precision metal components that consistently meet or exceed customer specifications and applicable regulatory requirements. This policy establishes our Quality Management System (QMS) framework in alignment with ISO 9001:2015 and AS9100 Rev D (Aerospace) requirements.

---

## 2. Quality Policy Statement

The leadership of Simplicit Demo Company commits to:
- Understanding and fulfilling customer requirements on every order
- Continuously improving our processes, products, and systems
- Empowering all employees to identify and resolve quality issues
- Meeting applicable statutory and regulatory requirements

Quality is every employee's responsibility.

---

## 3. QMS Scope

The QMS covers the design, development, manufacturing, inspection, and delivery of precision metal components at all Simplicit Demo Company facilities (Columbus HQ, Plant A, and Plant B).

---

## 4. Key Quality Principles

### 4.1 Customer Focus
Quality begins with a thorough understanding of customer requirements. Sales, Engineering, and Quality shall collaborate to translate customer specifications into internal control plans, inspection criteria, and production traveler requirements before production begins.

### 4.2 Process Approach
All processes affecting product quality must be documented, monitored, and controlled. Process owners are responsible for maintaining current procedures and responding to process deviations.

### 4.3 Nonconforming Product
All nonconforming material must be identified, segregated, and dispositioned through the NCR process (see PROC-QA-001). No nonconforming material may be shipped without documented customer deviation approval or rework verification.

### 4.4 Continual Improvement
The company commits to reducing defects, scrap, and customer escapes year-over-year. Annual quality objectives are established by the VP Quality and reviewed at Management Review.

### 4.5 Supplier Quality
Suppliers of production materials and services are subject to approval, performance monitoring, and regular scorecarding (see Quality/supplier_scorecard). Only approved suppliers may supply production materials.

---

## 5. Key Quality Metrics (Annual Targets)

| Metric | 2026 Target |
|--------|-------------|
| First Pass Yield | ≥ 97.0% |
| Customer PPM | ≤ 250 |
| On-Time Delivery | ≥ 95.0% |
| Scrap Rate | ≤ 2.5% |
| Supplier On-Time Delivery | ≥ 92.0% |
| Internal Audit Completion | 100% per schedule |

---

## 6. Management Review

The VP Quality shall conduct a formal Management Review of the QMS at least twice annually, reviewing quality performance data, audit results, customer feedback, nonconformance trends, and resource needs.

---

## 7. Document Control

All QMS documents (policies, procedures, work instructions, forms) are controlled documents subject to version control, approval, and distribution requirements. Obsolete documents must be removed from use.
