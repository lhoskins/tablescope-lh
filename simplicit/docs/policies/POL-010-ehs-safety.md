# POL-010: Environmental Health and Safety Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-010  
**Title:** Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** EHS Director  
**Scope:** All Simplicit Demo Company employees, contractors, and visitors at all facilities  

---

## 1. Purpose

Simplicit Demo Company is committed to providing a safe and healthy work environment and to operating in an environmentally responsible manner. This policy establishes our EHS commitments and expectations.

---

## 2. EHS Policy Statement

Simplicit Demo Company commits to:
- Protecting the health and safety of all employees, contractors, and visitors
- Complying with all applicable OSHA regulations and environmental laws
- Preventing work-related injuries, illnesses, and environmental incidents
- Continuously improving our EHS performance through training, inspection, and corrective action
- Empowering employees to stop work when an unsafe condition is identified

Safety is not subordinate to production. No production goal justifies compromising safety.

---

## 3. Key EHS Standards

### 3.1 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
Required PPE must be worn in all designated areas. PPE is provided at no cost to employees. Refusal to wear required PPE is subject to disciplinary action.

### 3.2 Hazard Communication
All hazardous chemicals must be properly labeled and accompanied by Safety Data Sheets (SDS). Employees working with hazardous chemicals must complete Hazmat training before first use.

### 3.3 Lockout/Tagout (LOTO)
Energy control procedures (LOTO) must be followed whenever performing maintenance on energized equipment. Unauthorized bypass of LOTO controls will result in immediate disciplinary action.

### 3.4 Incident Reporting
All incidents — including near-misses — must be reported within 24 hours using the EHS incident reporting system (see PROC-EHS-001). Prompt reporting enables corrective action and prevents recurrence.

### 3.5 Environmental Compliance
Waste generation, storage, and disposal must comply with EPA regulations. Spills of hazardous materials must be reported to EHS immediately. Annual environmental compliance audits are conducted by EHS.

### 3.6 Emergency Response
All facilities maintain Emergency Action Plans. Employees must know the evacuation routes, assembly points, and emergency contacts for their facility.

---

## 4. EHS Targets (2026)

| Metric | 2026 Target |
|--------|-------------|
| OSHA Recordable Incident Rate | ≤ 1.5 per 200K hours |
| Days Away from Work Rate | ≤ 0.5 |
| Near-Miss Reports | ≥ 48/year (encourage reporting culture) |
| EHS Training Compliance | ≥ 95% by required date |
| Environmental Violations | Zero |

---

## 5. Responsibilities

- **All Employees:** Follow EHS rules; report hazards and incidents; participate in training
- **Supervisors:** Enforce EHS standards; ensure team training completion; investigate incidents
- **EHS Director:** Develop programs; conduct audits; maintain regulatory compliance; report to leadership
- **Leadership:** Provide resources for EHS; model safe behaviors; include EHS in business reviews
