# POL-011: Procurement and Vendor Management Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-011  
**Title:** Procurement and Vendor Management Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Director of Procurement  
**Scope:** All purchasing activities and vendor relationships at Simplicit Demo Company  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the standards and controls governing procurement of goods and services to ensure competitive sourcing, ethical supplier relationships, appropriate authorization controls, and alignment with quality and EHS requirements.

---

## 2. Purchasing Authority

| Transaction Amount | Approval Required |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Up to $2,500 | Manager |
| $2,501 – $25,000 | Procurement + Director |
| $25,001 – $100,000 | Procurement + VP Operations |
| Over $100,000 | Procurement + CFO + CEO |

All purchases must be supported by a Purchase Order. Verbal or informal commitments to suppliers are not binding and should not be made without a PO.

---

## 3. Supplier Selection and Approval

New suppliers must be evaluated and approved before receiving purchase orders for production materials or critical services. The supplier approval process includes:
- Financial stability review
- Quality system assessment (ISO 9001 or equivalent for production suppliers)
- EHS assessment for chemical or hazardous material suppliers
- Conflict of interest disclosure from the requesting employee

Approved suppliers are maintained on the Approved Supplier List (ASL). Purchasing from non-approved suppliers requires written waiver from the Procurement Director.

---

## 4. Competitive Sourcing

- Purchases over $25,000 require at least three competitive quotes unless a sole-source justification is documented and approved
- Preferred supplier pricing may be used without re-quoting within 12 months of a competitive award
- Procurement must document all sourcing decisions and retain records for five years

---

## 5. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any personal or financial relationship with a current or prospective supplier. Employees with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves from vendor selection and contract decisions involving that supplier.

---

## 6. Supplier Performance Management

Active suppliers are assessed quarterly on delivery, quality, and pricing using the supplier scorecard (see data/quality/supplier_scorecard). Suppliers with consistently poor scores (overall < 70) are subject to corrective action requirements or removal from the ASL.

---

## 7. Contract Requirements

All supplier engagements over $10,000 or covering more than 90 days must be governed by a written agreement. Contracts must include:
- Quality and delivery requirements
- Inspection and right-to-audit rights
- Indemnification and limitation of liability
- Data security requirements (if applicable)
- Termination provisions

All contracts must be reviewed by Legal before execution.
