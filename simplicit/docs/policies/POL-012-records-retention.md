# POL-012: Records Retention Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-012  
**Title:** Records Retention and Disposition Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** General Counsel  
**Scope:** All company records in any format (electronic and physical)  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the minimum retention periods for company records to ensure compliance with legal requirements and support business operations, while avoiding unnecessary accumulation of records.

---

## 2. Retention Schedule (Selected Categories)

| Record Category | Retention Period | Authority |
|----------------|-----------------|-----------|
| Corporate formation documents | Permanent | Legal |
| Financial statements, tax returns | 7 years | IRS / SOX |
| Contracts | 7 years after expiration | Legal |
| Customer purchase orders | 7 years | Business |
| Employee records (active) | Duration of employment + 7 years | EEOC/FLSA |
| Payroll records | 7 years | FLSA |
| OSHA records (Form 300) | 5 years | OSHA |
| Environmental permits | Life of permit + 5 years | EPA |
| Quality records (production) | 10 years or customer requirement | ISO 9001 / AS9100 |
| Engineering drawings | Life of product + 10 years | Business |
| ITAR/export records | 5 years | EAR/ITAR |
| Email and general correspondence | 3 years | Business |
| IT system logs | 1 year | Security |
| Marketing materials | 3 years | Business |

---

## 3. Disposition

Records that have met their retention period and are not subject to a legal hold must be destroyed in a secure, appropriate manner:
- Electronic records: secure deletion or certified media destruction
- Physical documents: cross-cut shredding or certified shredding vendor

Records may not be disposed of if a legal hold is in effect (see Section 4).

---

## 4. Legal Holds

When litigation is reasonably anticipated or a government inquiry is received, Legal will issue a Legal Hold notice. Records subject to a legal hold must be preserved regardless of their normal retention period until Legal releases the hold.

---

## 5. Responsibilities

- **All Employees:** Maintain records per this schedule; respond to legal hold notices promptly
- **Records Custodians (dept. leads):** Ensure team compliance; manage disposal
- **Legal:** Maintain retention schedule; issue and manage legal holds
- **IT:** Implement automated retention controls in email and document management systems
