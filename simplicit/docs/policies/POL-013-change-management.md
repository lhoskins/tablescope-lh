# POL-013: Change Management Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-013  
**Title:** Change Management Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Chief Operating Officer  
**Scope:** All changes to production processes, IT systems, quality procedures, and engineering designs  

---

## 1. Purpose

Uncontrolled changes are a leading source of quality escapes, system outages, and production disruptions. This policy establishes minimum requirements for planning, approving, and implementing changes at Simplicit Demo Company.

---

## 2. Scope of Changes Covered

This policy applies to:
- **Engineering Changes:** Design changes to products, drawings, or specifications (see also ECR process)
- **Process Changes:** Changes to manufacturing processes, SOPs, or control plans
- **IT Changes:** Changes to production or critical business systems (infrastructure, software, configuration)
- **Quality Changes:** Changes to inspection methods, acceptance criteria, or QMS procedures
- **Supplier Changes:** Changes to production material suppliers or sub-tier suppliers

---

## 3. Change Categories

| Category | Description | Approval Required |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| **Major** | Potentially significant impact on product quality, delivery, cost, or system availability | Cross-functional CCB + VP level |
| **Minor** | Low risk; well-understood impact | Dept manager + Quality sign-off |
| **Emergency** | Urgent change to prevent immediate harm or outage | VP approval; full documentation within 24 hrs |

---

## 4. Change Process Requirements

All major and minor changes must:
1. Be documented in the change management system with description, rationale, and risk assessment
2. Receive required approvals before implementation
3. Include an implementation and rollback/recovery plan
4. Be verified after implementation to confirm the desired outcome
5. Be communicated to affected parties

Customer notification is required for changes affecting form, fit, or function of customer-specified product or controlled processes in aerospace or defense work orders.

---

## 5. IT Change Control

The IT Change Advisory Board (CAB) meets weekly to review and approve IT changes. Emergency changes may be approved by the CIO and IT Director without a full CAB meeting, with post-implementation review at the next CAB.

Production system maintenance windows are Saturdays 10 PM – 2 AM ET. Changes outside this window require COO approval.
