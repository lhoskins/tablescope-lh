# POL-014: Business Continuity Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-014  
**Title:** Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Chief Operating Officer  
**Scope:** All critical business functions and IT systems of Simplicit Demo Company  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Simplicit Demo Company's commitment to maintaining critical business operations during disruptive events and recovering systems and processes within defined objectives.

---

## 2. Recovery Objectives

| System / Process | Recovery Time Objective (RTO) | Recovery Point Objective (RPO) |
|-----------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| ERP (production scheduling) | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| Manufacturing execution | 4 hours | 2 hours |
| Email and communications | 2 hours | 4 hours |
| Financial systems | 8 hours | 1 hour |
| Engineering CAD/PDM | 24 hours | 4 hours |
| Production (manual fallback) | 2 hours | N/A |

---

## 3. Business Continuity Plans

Each department must maintain a documented Business Continuity Plan (BCP) reviewed at least annually. BCPs must identify critical functions, minimum staffing, alternate procedures, and escalation contacts.

BCPs are maintained on the IT intranet under "Business Continuity."

---

## 4. IT Disaster Recovery

The IT team maintains a Disaster Recovery (DR) plan including:
- Daily automated backups of all production systems to off-site encrypted storage
- Quarterly DR test of ERP and manufacturing systems
- Annual full-scale DR exercise including production simulation

---

## 5. Incident Command

A Business Continuity Incident Command Team (ICT) activates for significant disruptions:
- **Incident Commander:** COO
- **Operations Lead:** VP Manufacturing
- **IT Lead:** CIO
- **HR Lead:** CHRO
- **Communications:** Marketing/PR Director

The ICT meets within 2 hours of a declared continuity event and provides updates every 4 hours during active incidents.

---

## 6. Testing

BCPs and DR plans are tested at least annually. Results are documented and gaps addressed within 60 days.
