# POL-015: Data Privacy Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-015  
**Title:** Data Privacy Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** General Counsel / Chief Information Officer  
**Scope:** All personal data collected, processed, or stored by Simplicit Demo Company  

---

## 1. Purpose

Simplicit Demo Company collects personal data from employees, customers, suppliers, and website visitors in the course of doing business. This policy establishes how we collect, use, protect, retain, and dispose of personal data in compliance with applicable privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and relevant state privacy regulations.

---

## 2. Types of Personal Data Collected

- **Employee Data:** Name, contact information, government ID, salary, benefits, performance records, and health information (where applicable)
- **Customer/Contact Data:** Business contact information, purchasing history, communication preferences
- **Supplier Data:** Contact information, payment details, performance history
- **Website Visitors:** IP address, cookie data, usage analytics (see website Privacy Notice)

---

## 3. Principles of Data Processing

The company processes personal data in accordance with these principles:
- **Lawfulness:** We process data only with a valid legal basis (consent, contract, legitimate interest, legal obligation)
- **Purpose Limitation:** Data collected for one purpose will not be used for an incompatible purpose without notice or consent
- **Data Minimization:** We collect only the data necessary for the stated purpose
- **Accuracy:** We take reasonable steps to keep data accurate and up to date
- **Storage Limitation:** Data is retained per the Records Retention Policy (POL-012) and no longer than necessary
- **Security:** Appropriate technical and organizational measures protect data from unauthorized access or disclosure

---

## 4. Employee Data Rights

Employees may request access to their personal data held by HR. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days. Employees may also request correction of inaccurate data. Requests should be submitted to HR in writing.

---

## 5. Customer and Third-Party Data

Customer personal data is used solely for fulfilling orders, managing the customer relationship, and statutory compliance. It is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Customer data is shared with service providers only under data processing agreements requiring equivalent protections.

---

## 6. Data Breach Response

In the event of unauthorized access or disclosure of personal data, the company will:
1. Investigate and contain the breach immediately
2. Notify affected individuals as required by law (generally within 72 hours for EU data; per state law for US data)
3. Notify relevant regulators as required
4. Document the incident and corrective actions taken

---

## 7. Responsibilities

- **General Counsel:** Privacy compliance; breach notification; contracts with data processors
- **CIO:** Technical controls; system security; access management
- **HR:** Employee data stewardship; training
- **All Employees:** Handle personal data per this policy; report breaches immediately
