# PROC-EHS-001: Incident Reporting and Investigation Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-EHS-001  
**Title:** EHS Incident Reporting, Investigation, and Corrective Action  
**Department:** EHS  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** EHS Director  

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure all workplace incidents — including near-misses — are promptly reported, thoroughly investigated, and followed up with effective corrective actions to prevent recurrence.

---

## 2. Scope

All employees, contractors, and visitors at Simplicit Demo Company facilities.

---

## 3. What Must Be Reported

- All injuries or illnesses — no matter how minor (first aid only)
- Near-misses (unsafe acts or conditions that did not result in injury but could have)
- Property damage over $500
- Environmental releases or spills
- Security incidents

**Culture note:** Near-miss reporting is encouraged and celebrated, not penalized. A reported near-miss is a learning opportunity.

---

## 4. Reporting Procedure

### Immediate (Within 15 Minutes)
1. Ensure the injured employee receives appropriate medical care (call 911 for life-threatening emergencies)
2. Secure the scene to prevent secondary incidents; preserve evidence
3. Notify the Supervisor immediately
4. Supervisor notifies EHS within 15 minutes

### Within 24 Hours
5. Complete the EHS Incident Report form in the safety management system
6. For OSHA-recordable injuries: EHS Director reviews and determines recordability
7. For fatalities or inpatient hospitalizations: OSHA must be notified within 8 hours (contact EHS Director immediately)

---

## 5. Investigation

All incidents (including near-misses) require a documented investigation:

| Severity | Required Depth | Timeline |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| Near-miss / First Aid | Supervisor + EHS — 5-Why | 3 business days |
| OSHA Recordable | EHS + cross-functional team | 5 business days |
| Lost Time / Serious | EHS Director-led formal investigation | 7 business days |

Investigation outputs: root cause(s), contributing factors, corrective actions with owners and due dates.

---

## 6. Corrective Action Tracking

All corrective actions are tracked in the safety management system. Overdue corrective actions are reported to department VPs weekly by EHS. Corrective action completion is verified by EHS before the incident is closed.

---

## 7. OSHA Recordkeeping

EHS maintains the OSHA 300 Log. Annual summary (OSHA 300A) is posted February 1 – April 30 each year. Records are retained for 5 years per OSHA requirements.
