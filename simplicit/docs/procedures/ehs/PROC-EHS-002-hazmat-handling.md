# PROC-EHS-002: Hazardous Material Handling Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-EHS-002  
**Title:** Hazardous Material Receipt, Storage, Use, and Disposal  
**Department:** EHS  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** EHS Director  

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure hazardous materials are handled safely and in compliance with OSHA HazCom (29 CFR 1910.1200) and EPA regulations.

---

## 2. Scope

All hazardous chemicals used or stored at Simplicit Demo Company facilities.

---

## 3. Approved Chemicals

Only chemicals on the Approved Chemical List may be used in production or maintenance. Requests to add new chemicals must be submitted to EHS for SDS review and approval before purchase. Procurement will not order unapproved chemicals.

---

## 4. Labeling Requirements

All hazardous chemical containers must be labeled with:
- Chemical identity (product name)
- GHS hazard pictograms
- Signal word (Danger / Warning)
- Hazard statements
- Precautionary statements
- Supplier information

Labels must not be removed or defaced. Unlabeled containers must be quarantined and reported to EHS.

---

## 5. Storage Requirements

- Store chemicals per SDS requirements (temperature, ventilation, incompatibility)
- Flammable liquids must be stored in UL-listed flammable storage cabinets
- Acids and bases must be stored separately
- Maximum in-use quantities on the production floor: limited to daily use amounts
- Secondary containment required for liquid chemicals > 5 gallons

---

## 6. PPE Requirements

Required PPE for chemical handling is specified on the chemical SDS and the job-specific work instruction. Minimum requirements for any chemical handling:
- Safety glasses
- Chemical-resistant gloves (verify glove material compatibility with SDS)
- Closed-toe shoes

---

## 7. Spill Response

1. Evacuate the immediate area if spill is large or fumes are present
2. Notify Supervisor and EHS immediately
3. Use appropriate spill kit for cleanup (PPE required)
4. Do not wash spills down drains without EHS approval
5. Document spill in EHS incident system

---

## 8. Disposal

All hazardous waste must be disposed of through EHS-approved hazardous waste contractors. Hazardous waste may not be poured down drains or placed in regular trash. EHS manages manifests and EPA reporting.
