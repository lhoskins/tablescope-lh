# PROC-ENG-001: Design Review Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-ENG-001  
**Title:** Engineering Design Review Gate Process  
**Department:** Engineering  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Director of Engineering  

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure all new product designs and significant design changes are reviewed by a cross-functional team before release to production, reducing the risk of costly rework, scrap, or customer escapes.

---

## 2. Scope

All new product development projects and major design changes (ECRs affecting form, fit, or function) in the Engineering department.

---

## 3. Design Review Gates

| Gate | When | Focus |
|------|------|-------|
| **DRG-1: Concept Review** | After concept definition | Technical feasibility, customer requirements coverage, make/buy decision |
| **DRG-2: Preliminary Design Review (PDR)** | After preliminary design | Design vs. requirements traceability, initial BOM, risk identification |
| **DRG-3: Critical Design Review (CDR)** | Before first build | Detailed drawings, tolerance analysis, process plan, DFMEA complete |
| **DRG-4: First Article Review (FAR)** | After first article | Dimensional results, material certs, process capability data |
| **DRG-5: Production Readiness Review (PRR)** | Before full production | All documentation complete, capacity confirmed, lessons learned |

---

## 4. Participants (Required)

- Lead Engineer (presenter)
- Engineering Manager (chair)
- Quality Engineer
- Manufacturing Engineer
- Procurement Representative
- Customer representative (DRG-4 and DRG-5 for aerospace/defense)

---

## 5. Procedure Steps

### Before the Review
- Lead Engineer prepares design review package (drawings, DFMEA, BOM, open issues list) minimum 5 business days before meeting
- Package is distributed to all reviewers
- Reviewers submit written comments minimum 2 business days before meeting

### During the Review
- Lead Engineer presents design status and response to written comments
- Reviewers raise additional questions; all actions are captured in the gate action log
- Gate decision: **Pass**, **Pass with Conditions**, or **Fail** (re-review required)

### After the Review
- Engineering Manager documents gate decision in project tracking system within 24 hours
- Open actions are assigned owners and due dates
- "Pass with Conditions" gates: conditions must be verified closed before proceeding to next gate
