# PROC-ENG-002: Engineering Change Request (ECR) Process

**Procedure ID:** PROC-ENG-002  
**Title:** Engineering Change Request Initiation, Review, and Implementation  
**Department:** Engineering  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Director of Engineering  

---

## 1. Purpose

To manage engineering changes in a controlled manner that preserves product quality, customer satisfaction, and documentation accuracy.

---

## 2. Scope

All engineering changes to released drawings, specifications, BOMs, or process plans for production parts.

---

## 3. Definitions

- **ECR (Engineering Change Request):** A formal request to evaluate a proposed change
- **ECO (Engineering Change Order):** An approved instruction to implement a change
- **Affected Assemblies:** All products/assemblies using the part or document being changed

---

## 4. Procedure

### Step 1: Initiate ECR
Any employee may initiate an ECR. The initiator completes the ECR form in the engineering system, documenting:
- Affected part/document number(s)
- Description of proposed change
- Reason for change (defect, cost, customer request, obsolescence, etc.)
- Estimated cost impact

### Step 2: ECR Review
Engineering Manager assigns the ECR to a Lead Engineer for evaluation within 2 business days. The Lead Engineer assesses:
- Technical validity and feasibility
- Impact on form, fit, or function
- Affected assemblies and drawings
- Required testing or qualification
- Customer notification requirements

### Step 3: Cross-Functional Impact Review
ECRs affecting production parts require sign-off from Manufacturing, Quality, and Procurement before approval. High-cost or customer-affecting changes require VP Engineering approval.

### Step 4: ECO Release
Upon approval, the Engineering Change Order is issued, updating all affected documents. The ECO specifies the effective serial/lot number or date for production implementation.

### Step 5: Implementation Verification
Quality verifies the change is implemented correctly on the first production lot after ECO effective date.
