# PROC-EXC-001: Strategic Planning Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-EXC-001  
**Title:** Annual Strategic Planning Process  
**Department:** Executive  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Chief Executive Officer  

---

## 1. Purpose

To establish a repeatable, inclusive, and rigorous annual process for defining and refreshing the company's three-year strategic plan and linking it to the annual operating plan and budget.

---

## 2. Scope

All executive and VP-level leadership; facilitated by the CEO and supported by Finance/FP&A.

---

## 3. Strategic Planning Calendar

| Activity | Timeline |
|----------|----------|
| Environmental scan and competitive assessment | August |
| Customer and market feedback synthesis | August |
| VP-level strategic initiative proposals submitted | September 1 |
| Off-site leadership strategy session | Mid-September |
| Draft 3-year strategic plan circulated | October 1 |
| Board strategy session | October Board Meeting |
| Final strategic plan approved | November 1 |
| Strategic plan cascaded to department objectives | November |
| Linked to budget (PROC-FIN-002) | November–December |

---

## 4. Procedure Steps

### Step 1: Environmental Scan (August)
CFO and VP Sales compile: market growth forecasts, competitive landscape changes, customer concentration analysis, regulatory developments, macroeconomic factors affecting the business.

### Step 2: Initiative Proposals (September 1)
Each VP submits up to 3 strategic initiative proposals, each including: problem statement, proposed solution, 3-year financial impact, resource requirements, key risks, and recommended KPIs.

### Step 3: Off-Site Strategy Session (2 days)
CEO facilitates. Agenda:
- Day 1: Review environmental scan; present and debate initiatives; prioritize by strategic fit and ROI
- Day 2: Draft strategic priorities (3-5 for the 3-year period); assign initiative owners; define success metrics

### Step 4: Plan Documentation
CEO and CFO produce the written Strategic Plan document including: vision/mission reaffirmation, 3-year financial framework, strategic priorities, initiative roadmap, and governance model for tracking.

### Step 5: Board Review
Strategic plan is presented to the Board for input and endorsement at the October Board meeting.

### Step 6: Cascade and Alignment
Approved priorities are cascaded to VP-level objectives, then to department-level goals and individual performance objectives.

---

## 5. Mid-Year Review

A mid-year strategy review is conducted in July:
- Initiative progress assessed vs. milestones
- Significant market changes evaluated
- Priorities may be adjusted with CEO and Board Chair concurrence
