# PROC-EXC-002: Board Reporting Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-EXC-002  
**Title:** Board of Directors Reporting and Meeting Preparation  
**Department:** Executive  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Chief Executive Officer / General Counsel  

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure the Board of Directors receives accurate, timely, and appropriately formatted information to fulfill their governance responsibilities.

---

## 2. Board Meeting Schedule

The Board of Directors meets quarterly (March, June, October, December). Special sessions may be called by the Board Chair or CEO with 10-day notice.

---

## 3. Standard Board Package Contents

Each quarterly board package includes:

| Section | Owner | Due to CEO |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| CEO Letter — strategic narrative and key issues | CEO | T-7 days |
| Financial Statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow) | CFO | T-5 days |
| Budget vs. Actual and Full-Year Forecast | CFO | T-5 days |
| Operational Dashboard (KPIs) | COO | T-5 days |
| Sales Pipeline and Bookings Report | VP Sales | T-5 days |
| Risk Register Update | General Counsel | T-5 days |
| HR Dashboard (Headcount, Turnover, Open Positions) | CHRO | T-5 days |
| Strategic Initiative Status | Initiative Owners | T-7 days |
| Legal and Compliance Update | General Counsel | T-5 days |

Board package is distributed to Board members T-3 business days before the meeting.

---

## 4. Meeting Preparation

### Executive Pre-Read
CEO and General Counsel review the complete package for consistency and accuracy T-4 days before the meeting. Any material inconsistencies are corrected before distribution.

### Management Presentation
CEO presents for 60 minutes; CFO, COO, and CHRO present for 20 minutes each. Presentations follow the standard template maintained on the Executive SharePoint.

### Q&A Preparation
Each presenter prepares answers to likely Board questions. Legal briefs the CEO on any pending legal matters before the meeting.

---

## 5. Minutes and Action Items

General Counsel (or designee) records formal minutes. Action items are captured, assigned, and distributed within 3 business days of the meeting. Status of prior meeting action items is reviewed at each subsequent meeting.
