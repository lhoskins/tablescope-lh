# PROC-FIN-001: Month-End Close Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-FIN-001  
**Title:** Month-End Financial Close  
**Department:** Finance  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Controller  
**References:** POL-012 (Records Retention), GL Summary Monthly data  

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure the timely, accurate, and complete close of the general ledger each month, enabling accurate financial reporting and analysis.

---

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers all GL close activities for Simplicit Demo Company's operating entities, performed by the Finance team within the first five business days of each month.

---

## 3. Definitions

- **Close Period:** The calendar month being closed
- **Accrual:** An estimated expense or revenue recognized in the period incurred/earned, prior to the actual invoice
- **Hard Close:** GL period is locked; no further entries permitted without Controller approval
- **Flux Analysis:** Comparison of actuals vs. prior period or budget with explanation of material variances

---

## 4. Procedure Steps

### Day 1 (First Business Day After Month End)

| Step | Task | Owner |
|------|------|-------|
| 1.1 | Download AP invoice file from ERP; confirm all invoices with service dates in close period are posted | AP Clerk |
| 1.2 | Receive and review payroll journal entry from HR/Payroll; post to GL | Payroll Accountant |
| 1.3 | Post all bank transactions; begin bank reconciliation | Staff Accountant |
| 1.4 | Issue close task checklist to all department accountants | Controller |

### Day 2

| Step | Task | Owner |
|------|------|-------|
| 2.1 | Post depreciation journal entries from fixed asset sub-ledger | Fixed Asset Accountant |
| 2.2 | Post all accruals for known expenses not yet invoiced (maintenance, utilities, professional fees) | Staff Accountant |
| 2.3 | Confirm inventory counts are reconciled to system; post any inventory adjustments | Cost Accountant |
| 2.4 | Close accounts receivable sub-ledger; confirm AR aging agrees to GL | AR Specialist |

### Day 3

| Step | Task | Owner |
|------|------|-------|
| 3.1 | Complete bank reconciliations for all accounts | Staff Accountant |
| 3.2 | Reconcile intercompany accounts (if applicable) | Controller |
| 3.3 | Run trial balance; perform initial flux analysis vs. prior month and budget | FP&A Analyst |
| 3.4 | Review and approve all journal entries over $10,000 | Controller |

### Day 4

| Step | Task | Owner |
|------|------|-------|
| 4.1 | Address open items from flux review; post any corrections | Staff Accountant |
| 4.2 | Prepare department variance reports for distribution | FP&A Analyst |
| 4.3 | Hard close GL period in ERP | Controller |

### Day 5

| Step | Task | Owner |
|------|------|-------|
| 5.1 | Finalize and distribute monthly financial package to department heads | FP&A Analyst |
| 5.2 | Submit financial data to executive dashboard (KPI update) | FP&A Analyst |
| 5.3 | Archive close documentation per retention policy | Controller |

---

## 5. Escalation

Material errors discovered after hard close require Controller and CFO approval for an adjusting journal entry. Errors affecting revenue by more than $50,000 or any balance sheet account by more than $100,000 must be communicated to the CFO on the day identified.
