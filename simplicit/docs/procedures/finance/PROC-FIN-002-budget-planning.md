# PROC-FIN-002: Annual Budget Planning Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-FIN-002  
**Title:** Annual Budget Planning and Approval  
**Department:** Finance  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** CFO / FP&A Director  

---

## 1. Purpose

To produce a complete, accurate, and leadership-approved annual operating budget aligned with the company's strategic objectives.

---

## 2. Scope

This procedure covers the annual budgeting process for all departments, conducted each fall for the following fiscal year.

## 4. Procedure Steps

### Step 1: Executive Guidance
CFO issues a guidance memo stating revenue targets, key strategic investments, headcount freeze/growth guidelines, and capital budget limits for the planning cycle.

### Step 2: Department Submissions
Each department head submits a detailed budget by cost center and account category. Submissions must include:
- Personnel plan (headcount by role, start date, salary)
- Non-personnel expenses by GL account
- Capital expenditure requests with business justification
- Key assumptions and risks

### Step 3: FP&A Consolidation
FP&A consolidates all submissions into the company financial model, checks for mathematical accuracy, and performs variance analysis vs. prior year and current-year forecast.

### Step 4: Challenge Sessions
CFO meets with each department head to challenge material increases, validate headcount plans, and identify savings opportunities. Minutes are documented.

### Step 5: Approval and Publication
Final budget is approved by the CEO and Board. FP&A loads the approved budget into the ERP and distributes the budget package to all department heads.
