# PROC-HR-001: New Employee Onboarding Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-HR-001  
**Title:** New Employee Onboarding  
**Department:** Human Resources  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** HR Director  

---

## 1. Purpose

To provide a consistent, welcoming, and effective onboarding experience that equips new employees with the knowledge, tools, and relationships needed to become productive members of Simplicit Demo Company.

---

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all new full-time and part-time employees. Contractor onboarding follows a separate abbreviated process managed by IT.

---

## 3. Pre-Start (1-2 Weeks Before Start Date)

| Task | Owner |
|------|-------|
| Send offer letter and confirm start details | HR |
| Initiate background check | HR |
| Submit IT new-user request (see PROC-IT-001) | Hiring Manager |
| Order PPE and any specialized equipment (manufacturing roles) | EHS / Supervisor |
| Assign onboarding buddy from same team | Hiring Manager |
| Prepare first-week schedule | Hiring Manager |
| Send welcome email with first-day instructions | HR |

---

## 4. Day 1

| Task | Owner |
|------|-------|
| Welcome and facility tour | HR |
| Badge and parking access provisioned | Facilities/IT |
| I-9 verification and paperwork completion | HR |
| Benefits enrollment overview (30-day enrollment window) | HR |
| IT systems access confirmed (laptop, email, ERP) | IT |
| Lunch with manager and team | Hiring Manager |
| EHS orientation and safety walkthrough (all plant employees) | EHS |
| Introduction to onboarding buddy | Hiring Manager |

---

## 5. Week 1

| Task | Owner |
|------|-------|
| Complete mandatory training: Code of Conduct (HR-002), Anti-Harassment (HR-001), Info Security (IT-001), EHS Safety Fundamentals (EHS-001) | Employee / HR |
| Meet with key cross-functional contacts | Manager |
| Review role expectations and first 90-day goals | Manager |
| Complete HR onboarding checklist in HRIS | Employee / HR |

---

## 6. 30 / 60 / 90-Day Check-ins

HR schedules structured check-in conversations at 30, 60, and 90 days to assess integration, identify issues early, and collect feedback on the onboarding experience. Manager also conducts 30-day and 90-day goal check-ins.

---

## 7. Manufacturing-Specific Requirements

New production employees must additionally complete:
- Forklift certification (if role requires) — EHS-002
- LOTO awareness training
- Line-specific SOP review with supervisor
- First week shadowing on production line before solo operation
