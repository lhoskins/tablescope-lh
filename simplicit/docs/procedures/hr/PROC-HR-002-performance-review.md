# PROC-HR-002: Annual Performance Review Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-HR-002  
**Title:** Annual Performance Review and Goal Setting  
**Department:** Human Resources  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** HR Director  

---

## 1. Purpose

To provide a fair, consistent, and development-oriented annual performance review process that supports employee growth and aligns individual performance with company objectives.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all full-time and part-time employees who have completed 6 months of service by December 31. New employees in their first 6 months complete a probationary review instead.

---

## 3. Annual Performance Calendar

| Activity | Timeline |
|----------|----------|
| Goal-setting kickoff for new year | January 15 |
| Mid-year check-in conversations | July 1–31 |
| Self-assessments open in HRIS | November 15 |
| Manager reviews due | December 1 |
| Calibration sessions (dept by dept) | December 8–19 |
| Ratings communicated to employees | January 10 |
| Merit increases effective | February 1 payroll |

---

## 4. Rating Scale

| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 5 – Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all expectations; significant impact beyond role |
| 4 – Exceeds Expectations | Frequently exceeds key expectations; high performer |
| 3 – Meets Expectations | Consistently meets role expectations; solid performer |
| 2 – Needs Improvement | Partially meets expectations; improvement plan required |
| 1 – Unsatisfactory | Fails to meet expectations; immediate action plan required |

---

## 5. Calibration Process

HR facilitates calibration sessions with each department head and their managers to ensure consistency in ratings across the company. HR will flag statistical outliers (e.g., departments with >40% "Exceeds" ratings) for re-review.

---

## 6. Merit Increase Guidelines

Merit increases are approved by the CFO based on company performance. HR provides a merit matrix grid (rating × position-in-band) to managers for reference. All merit decisions require VP approval.
