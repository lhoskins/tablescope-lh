# PROC-LEG-001: Contract Review and Approval Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-LEG-001  
**Title:** Contract Review, Negotiation, and Execution  
**Department:** Legal  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** General Counsel  

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure all contracts entered into by Simplicit Demo Company are legally sound, properly authorized, and appropriately protect the company's interests.

---

## 2. Scope

All contracts, agreements, and legally binding documents — including customer contracts, supplier agreements, NDAs, IP licenses, and service agreements — to which Simplicit Demo Company is a party.

---

## 3. Signature Authority

| Contract Value / Type | Authorized Signatory |
|----------------------|---------------------|
| NDAs, standard supply agreements < $10K | Dept Director |
| Agreements $10K – $100K | VP-level |
| Agreements $100K – $500K | COO or CFO |
| Agreements > $500K | CEO |
| Customer Master Agreements | CEO + General Counsel |
| IP Licenses, Joint Ventures | CEO + Board notification |

No employee may sign a contract on behalf of the company without this authorization. Verbal commitments are not binding agreements.

---

## 4. Contract Review Process

### Step 1: Initial Submission
The business owner submits the contract to Legal with a Contract Review Request, including:
- Business purpose and counterparty overview
- Key commercial terms and context
- Desired execution date
- Any areas of known risk or concern

### Step 2: Legal Review
Legal reviews the contract within the agreed SLA:
- Standard NDA: 3 business days
- Standard supply/customer agreement: 5 business days
- Complex or high-value agreement: 10 business days (scope agreed with business owner)

Legal redlines the contract and provides a summary of key risk areas.

### Step 3: Negotiation
Legal coordinates negotiation with the counterparty, keeping the business owner informed of material changes. All substantive changes must be reviewed by Legal before the business owner responds.

### Step 4: Approval and Execution
Upon final agreement on terms, Legal prepares a signature-ready version. The authorized signatory signs. Fully executed agreements are stored in the contract management system.

---

## 5. Prohibited Clauses

The following clauses require escalation to General Counsel before acceptance:
- Unlimited liability or indemnification obligations
- IP ownership transfers to counterparty
- Automatic renewal > 1 year
- Governing law in a foreign jurisdiction for material agreements
- Export control compliance waivers
