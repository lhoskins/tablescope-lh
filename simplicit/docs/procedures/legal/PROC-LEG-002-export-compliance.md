# PROC-LEG-002: Export Compliance Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-LEG-002  
**Title:** Export Control Compliance — EAR and ITAR Screening  
**Department:** Legal  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** General Counsel / Export Compliance Officer  

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure Simplicit Demo Company complies with U.S. export control regulations, including the Export Administration Regulations (EAR) and International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR), when exporting products, data, or technology.

---

## 2. Scope

All employees involved in shipping products internationally, sharing technical data with foreign nationals (including those in the U.S.), or hiring foreign nationals for roles involving controlled technology.

---

## 3. Key Regulations

- **EAR (15 CFR 730-774):** Administered by BIS; covers dual-use goods and technologies
- **ITAR (22 CFR 120-130):** Administered by DDTC; covers defense articles, defense services, and related technical data
- **OFAC Sanctions:** Restrictions on transactions with sanctioned countries, entities, and individuals

---

## 4. Product Classification

All products and components must be classified before export:
- Export Control Classification Number (ECCN) under EAR
- U.S. Munitions List (USML) category under ITAR (if applicable)

Engineering and Legal coordinate product classifications. Classifications are maintained in the product master database.

---

## 5. Screening Requirements

### Customer and Counterparty Screening
All new customers, suppliers, and business partners must be screened against:
- BIS Denied Persons List and Entity List
- OFAC SDN List and Consolidated Sanctions List
- State Dept. Debarred Parties List

Screening is performed during customer/supplier onboarding and annually thereafter.

### Order Screening
Each export order must be reviewed for:
- End-use and end-user concerns (prohibited: weapons of mass destruction, unauthorized military use)
- Country restrictions and embargoes (Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, others per current OFAC guidance)
- License requirements

---

## 6. Export Licenses

If a license is required, Legal submits the license application to the relevant agency. No export may occur until the license is received, unless a license exception applies. License exceptions must be documented.

---

## 7. Employee Training

All employees involved in export activities must complete export compliance training annually. New employees in affected roles must complete training before handling any export transactions.

---

## 8. Violations and Reporting

Suspected export control violations must be reported to the Export Compliance Officer immediately. Voluntary self-disclosure to the relevant agency may be appropriate and is evaluated case by case by Legal.
