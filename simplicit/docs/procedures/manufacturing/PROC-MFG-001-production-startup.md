# PROC-MFG-001: Production Line Startup Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-MFG-001  
**Title:** Production Line Daily Startup and Pre-Shift Verification  
**Department:** Manufacturing  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** VP Manufacturing  

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure all production lines are safely and correctly started at the beginning of each shift, minimizing downtime, scrap, and safety incidents.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all production lines (L1–L4) at Plant A and Plant B, for all shifts (1st, 2nd, 3rd).

---

## 3. Definitions

- **Shift Lead:** The designee responsible for production operations during a given shift
- **Production Traveler:** The work order document that travels with each production job
- **First Article:** The first part produced at startup, inspected to verify setup

---

## 4. Pre-Shift Startup Procedure

### Step 1: Safety Check (15 min before shift start)
- Walk the line; confirm no safety hazards, unauthorized personnel, or obstructions
- Verify all safety guards are in place and functional
- Check fire extinguisher and first-aid kit are present and unobstructed
- Confirm LOTO tags have been removed (if any maintenance was performed overnight)

### Step 2: Equipment Check
- Review maintenance log for any overnight or prior-shift issues
- Verify coolant/lubricant levels are within spec
- Confirm all tooling is installed and torqued per setup sheet
- Warm up CNC or precision equipment per manufacturer specification (minimum 15 minutes)

### Step 3: Material Verification
- Confirm raw material lot number matches the work order and is on the Approved Material List
- Verify material certs are on file in Quality system for aerospace and defense jobs
- Inspect incoming material for visible damage; reject and notify Procurement if found

### Step 4: First Article Inspection
- Produce a minimum of 3 first-article parts before releasing the job to full production
- Submit first articles to Quality Inspector for dimensional verification against drawing
- Do not run production until Quality confirms First Article Acceptance

### Step 5: Documentation
- Sign off startup checklist in production system
- Log any issues or deviations in the shift log
- Brief incoming operator on job status, known issues, and any open action items

---

## 5. Abnormal Startup Conditions

If any step cannot be completed or reveals an issue:
1. Notify Shift Supervisor immediately
2. Do not start production until the issue is resolved or Supervisor provides written authorization to proceed with deviation
3. Document the deviation in the shift log and NCR system if product quality may be affected
