# PROC-QA-001: Nonconforming Material Control Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-QA-001  
**Title:** Identification, Segregation, and Disposition of Nonconforming Material  
**Department:** Quality  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** VP Quality  

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure nonconforming material is promptly identified, segregated from conforming product, and dispositioned to prevent unintended use or delivery to customers.

---

## 2. Scope

All nonconforming material discovered during receiving inspection, in-process inspection, final inspection, or identified through customer feedback.

---

## 3. Definitions

- **Nonconforming Material:** Any material, part, or product that does not meet specified requirements
- **NCR (Nonconformance Report):** The formal record of nonconforming material
- **MRB (Material Review Board):** The cross-functional team authorized to disposition nonconforming material
- **Disposition:** The decision for how to handle NCR material (Scrap, Rework, Use As-Is, Return to Supplier)

---

## 4. Procedure

### Step 1: Identification
Any employee who identifies nonconforming material shall stop production or use immediately and notify the Quality Inspector.

### Step 2: Segregation
The Quality Inspector places a red "HOLD" tag on the affected material and moves it to the designated NCR Hold Area. Material must remain segregated until dispositioned. Accessing hold material without MRB authorization is prohibited.

### Step 3: NCR Documentation
The Quality Inspector opens an NCR in the quality system within 4 hours of identification, documenting:
- Part number, lot number, quantity affected
- Description of the nonconformance
- Detection method (inspection, customer complaint, etc.)
- Line/process where nonconformance originated

### Step 4: MRB Disposition
The MRB (Quality Engineer, Manufacturing Engineer, and Engineering as needed) reviews the NCR and assigns disposition:
- **Scrap:** Unrecoverable; destroy and record cost
- **Rework:** Define rework instructions; re-inspect after rework before use
- **Use As-Is (UAI):** Engineering evaluation confirms product meets functional requirements despite the nonconformance; customer deviation required for customer-specific requirements
- **Return to Supplier (RTS):** For purchased material; Procurement coordinates return

### Step 5: Closure
NCR is closed in the quality system after disposition is complete and verified. All cost data is captured for monthly reporting.

---

## 5. Customer Escapes

Any nonconformance discovered after product has shipped to a customer (customer escape) requires immediate notification to VP Quality, VP Sales, and the COO. Customer notification and corrective action plan are initiated within 24 hours.
