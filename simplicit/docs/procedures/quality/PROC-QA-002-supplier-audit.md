# PROC-QA-002: Supplier Quality Audit Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-QA-002  
**Title:** Supplier Quality Audit Procedure  
**Department:** Quality  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** VP Quality  

---

## 1. Purpose

To evaluate supplier quality systems and capabilities to ensure continued supply of conforming material and services.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to production material suppliers on the Approved Supplier List (ASL), prioritized by spend, criticality, and quality performance.

---

## 3. Audit Frequency

| Supplier Category | Audit Frequency |
|------------------|----------------|
| Critical (sole-source, aerospace, defense) | Annually |
| Standard (high spend or quality risk) | Every 2 years |
| Low Risk (low spend, no quality issues) | Every 3 years or as needed |

Suppliers with an overall scorecard score below 75 in any quarter are automatically scheduled for an audit within 90 days.

---

## 4. Audit Procedure

### Step 1: Planning
Quality Engineer issues an audit notification letter to the supplier minimum 30 days before the audit date. The letter specifies audit scope, date, expected duration, and documentation requests.

### Step 2: Document Review
Quality Engineer reviews supplier quality manual, certifications (ISO 9001, AS9100), recent corrective actions, and process documentation before the on-site visit.

### Step 3: On-Site Audit
Audit includes: quality system walkthrough, process observation, records review, personnel interviews, and a review of any outstanding corrective action items from prior audits.

### Step 4: Findings and Closeout
- Findings are documented as Major (system-level failure), Minor (isolated issue), or Observation (improvement opportunity)
- Audit report issued to supplier within 10 business days
- Major findings require supplier corrective action plan within 30 days and verification within 90 days
- Suppliers with unresolved major findings are placed on a supply hold pending resolution
