# PROC-SAL-001: Order Management Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-SAL-001  
**Title:** Customer Order Entry, Confirmation, and Fulfillment  
**Department:** Sales  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** VP Sales  

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure customer orders are accurately entered, acknowledged, and fulfilled in a manner that meets customer expectations and contractual commitments.

---

## 2. Scope

All customer purchase orders received by Simplicit Demo Company, regardless of channel (electronic, portal, email, fax).

---

## 3. Order Entry

### Step 1: Receipt and Log
All incoming purchase orders are directed to the Sales Operations inbox. Sales Operations logs receipt within 2 business hours and assigns an internal order number in the ERP.

### Step 2: Order Review
Sales Operations reviews the PO against the current quote and customer contract:
- Part numbers and descriptions match
- Quantities and pricing are as quoted
- Requested ship date is achievable (verify with Manufacturing)
- Terms and conditions are acceptable (flag non-standard T&Cs to Legal)

### Step 3: Order Acknowledgment
Customer receives an Order Acknowledgment within 1 business day confirming:
- Accepted line items, quantities, and pricing
- Confirmed ship date (or advisory if date requires adjustment)
- Internal order number for reference

---

## 4. Fulfillment and Shipping

- Manufacturing is notified of the order via work order in the MES system
- Sales Operations monitors order status weekly and alerts customer proactively if any delay is anticipated
- All shipments are accompanied by packing list, Certificate of Conformance (signed by Quality), and any customer-required documentation
- Shipping notification with tracking number is sent to customer same day as shipment

---

## 5. Order Exceptions

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Price discrepancy | Hold order; Sales Rep resolves with customer before entry |
| Delivery date unachievable | Notify customer immediately; propose alternative; document |
| Non-standard T&Cs | Route to Legal for review before acknowledgment |
| ITAR/export-controlled order | Notify Compliance before acknowledgment |
