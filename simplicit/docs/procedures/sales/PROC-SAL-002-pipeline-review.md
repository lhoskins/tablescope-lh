# PROC-SAL-002: Sales Pipeline Review Procedure

**Procedure ID:** PROC-SAL-002  
**Title:** Weekly Sales Pipeline Review and Forecast Update  
**Department:** Sales  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** VP Sales  

---

## 1. Purpose

To maintain an accurate and current sales pipeline that supports reliable revenue forecasting and proactive management of booking risk.

---

## 2. Scope

All active opportunities in the CRM (Salesforce) managed by the Simplicit Demo Company Sales team.

---

## 3. Pipeline Hygiene Standards

All opportunities must have:
- Accurate stage, probability, and expected close date (updated within the past 7 days)
- A next action with a date assigned
- A clear value based on most recent quote or estimate
- Notes from the most recent customer interaction (within 14 days for Proposal/Negotiation stage)

Opportunities not updated within 14 days are flagged as "stale" in the CRM dashboard.

---

## 4. Weekly Pipeline Review (Every Monday)

### Preparation (Friday before)
- Each Sales Rep updates all opportunities before Friday EOD
- Sales Operations pulls pipeline report from CRM and distributes to VP Sales

### Monday Review Meeting (60 min)
- VP Sales and all Sales Reps attend
- Review opportunities in Proposal and Negotiation stages in detail
- Identify opportunities at risk; assign corrective actions
- Update monthly forecast in CRM based on pipeline snapshot

---

## 5. Monthly Forecast Submission

By the 3rd business day of each month, Sales Operations submits the monthly bookings forecast to Finance, broken down by product line and customer segment. The forecast must include:
- Committed (>75% probability): monthly sum
- Likely (50-75%): monthly sum
- Upside (<50%): monthly sum
